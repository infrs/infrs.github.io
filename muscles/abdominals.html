Best abs exercises

Hanging Leg Raise, Cable Crunch and Ab Roller are among the best exercises for the abs. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the abs.

What the abs muscles do

The rectus abdominis, the six-pack, runs down the front of the belly, and the obliques wrap around the sides. They bend and twist the trunk, and brace it to protect the spine when you lift, push or carry. Visible abs depend more on body fat than on training.

Muscles of the abs

  • Upper rectus abdominisThe six-pack above the navel
  • Lower rectus abdominisThe six-pack below the navel
  • External obliqueSides of the waist
  • Internal obliqueUnder the external oblique; twists and bends the trunk
  • Transversus abdominisThe deepest layer of the belly; braces the trunk like a belt
  • IliopsoasPsoas and iliacus, deep in the belly and the front of the hip; lifts the thigh
