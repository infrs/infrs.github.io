Best calves & shins exercises

Standing Calf Raises, Seated Calf Raise and Single-Leg Calf Raise are among the best exercises for the calves & shins. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the calves & shins.

What the calves & shins muscles do

The gastrocnemius and soleus join into the Achilles tendon and point the foot, pushing you off the ground with every step. The fibularis muscles on the outside steady the ankle, and the tibialis anterior on the shin lifts the foot.

Muscles of the calves & shins

  • Gastrocnemius (medial head)Inner upper calf; works most with straight knees
  • Gastrocnemius (lateral head)Outer upper calf; works most with straight knees
  • SoleusUnder the gastrocnemius; works most with bent knees
  • Fibularis longusOuter lower leg (also called peroneus longus)
  • Fibularis brevisOuter lower leg, near the ankle
  • Tibialis anteriorFront of the shin; lifts the foot
  • Tibialis posteriorDeep in the back of the calf; holds up the arch of the foot
