Bent-Over Underhand Dumbbell Row

Equipment
Dumbbell
Level
Beginner
Type
Strength
Movement
Compound (several joints)
Force
Pull

Especially targets

  • Middle trapeziusTransverse part, across the shoulder blades
  • Rhomboid majorBetween the shoulder blades, under the trapezius
  • Rhomboid minorAbove the rhomboid major, under the trapezius

Main muscles

  • Middle back

Also works

  • Lats
  • Biceps
  • Forearms & hands

How to do it

  1. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with your palms facing forward. Bend your knees a little and hinge at the hips until your back is nearly level.
  2. Let the dumbbells hang under your shoulders with your back flat.
  3. Row the dumbbells up to your lower ribs, elbows close to your body, and squeeze your shoulder blades together.
  4. Lower them slowly until your arms are straight.
