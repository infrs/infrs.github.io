Jumping Jacks

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Beginner
Type
Cardio
Movement
Compound (several joints)

Main muscles

  • Quads

Also works

  • Calves & shins
  • Shoulders
  • Glutes

How to do it

  1. Stand with your feet together and arms by your sides.
  2. Jump your feet out wide while raising your arms overhead.
  3. Jump back to the start.
  4. Keep a steady rhythm.
