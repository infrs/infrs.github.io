Side-Lying Hip Adduction

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Beginner
Type
Strength
Movement
Isolation (one joint)
Force
Pull

Main muscles

  • Inner thighs

How to do it

  1. Lie on your side. Bend your top leg and plant its foot on the floor in front of your bottom knee.
  2. Keep your bottom leg straight.
  3. Lift the bottom leg as high as you can, squeezing your inner thigh.
  4. Lower slowly. Finish all reps, then switch sides.
