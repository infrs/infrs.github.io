Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift

Equipment
Dumbbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Especially targets

  • Biceps femoris (long head)Outer hamstring
  • SemimembranosusInner hamstring, under the semitendinosus

Main muscles

  • Hamstrings
  • Glutes

Also works

  • Lower back
  • Forearms & hands

How to do it

  1. This exercise involves holding a dumbbell in each hand and bending forward at the hips while keeping the back straight, then returning to a standing position.
  2. It primarily targets the hamstrings and glutes.
