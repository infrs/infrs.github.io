Teres major: what it does and how to train it

Below the shoulder blade; works with the lats. The best exercises for it include Straight-Arm Pulldown, Pullups and One-Arm Dumbbell Row.

What it does

Runs from the lower corner of the shoulder blade to the upper arm, beside the lats, which is why it’s nicknamed the lat’s little helper. It pulls the arm down and back and turns it in.

Best exercises for the teres major

  1. Straight-Arm PulldownCable · beginner · isolation
  2. PullupsOther · intermediate · compound
  3. One-Arm Dumbbell RowDumbbell · beginner · compound
  4. Band Assisted Pull-UpOther · beginner · compound
  5. Cable Incline PushdownCable · beginner · isolation
  6. Catch and Overhead ThrowMedicine ball · beginner · compound
