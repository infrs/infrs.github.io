How to do it

  1. "The Y" Position: Set an incline bench to 30-45 degrees.
  2. Lie face-down with your chest supported, keeping your head neutral or looking slightly down to avoid neck strain.
  3. Grip: Hold light dumbbells with a neutral or thumbs-up grip (thumb pointing to the ceiling).
  4. Movement: With straight or slightly bent elbows, raise the dumbbells up and out in a "Y" shape.
  5. Focus on moving the shoulders and shoulder blades rather than just lifting the arms.
  6. Top Position: Squeeze the shoulder blades together and reach high, ensuring the arms are angled to form a "Y".
  7. Control: Lower the dumbbells slowly to the starting position.
