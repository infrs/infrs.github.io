Flexor carpi radialis: what it does and how to train it

Bends the wrist, thumb side. The best exercises for it include Palms-Up Barbell Wrist Curl Over A Bench, Palms-Up Dumbbell Wrist Curl Over A Bench and Cable Wrist Curl.

What it does

Runs down the inner forearm to the thumb side of the wrist. It bends the wrist and tilts it toward the thumb; wrist curls train it.
