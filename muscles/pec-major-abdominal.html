Lower pectoralis major: what it does and how to train it

Abdominal part, along the lower edge of the chest. The best exercises for it include Dips - Chest Version, Decline Barbell Bench Press and Cable Crossover.

What it does

The lowest fibers of the chest, coming up from the sheath around the abs. They pull the arm down and across, so dips, decline presses and high-to-low cable crossovers work them most.

Best exercises for the lower pectoralis major

  3. Cable CrossoverCable · beginner · isolation
