Rhomboid major: what it does and how to train it

Between the shoulder blades, under the trapezius. The best exercises for it include Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row, Seated Cable Rows and Bent Over Barbell Row.

What it does

Lies between the shoulder blades, under the trapezius. It pulls the shoulder blades together and holds them against the ribs, keeping the shoulders back.

Best exercises for the rhomboid major

  1. Chest-Supported Dumbbell RowDumbbell · beginner · compound
  2. Seated Cable RowsCable · beginner · compound
  3. Bent Over Barbell RowBarbell · beginner · compound
  4. Face PullCable · intermediate · compound
