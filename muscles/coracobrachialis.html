Coracobrachialis: what it does and how to train it

Inner upper arm, near the armpit. The best exercises for it include Barbell Curl, Incline Dumbbell Curl and Hammer Curls.

What it does

A small muscle high on the inner arm, from the shoulder blade to the upper-arm bone. It helps pull the arm forward and toward the body, and works in chest flyes and presses.

Best exercises for the coracobrachialis

  1. Barbell CurlBarbell · beginner · isolation
  2. Incline Dumbbell CurlDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  3. Hammer CurlsDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  4. Preacher CurlBarbell · beginner · isolation
  5. Bayesian Cable CurlCable · beginner · isolation
