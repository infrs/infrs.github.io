Biceps femoris (short head): what it does and how to train it

Outer hamstring, lower part; only bends the knee. The best exercises for it include Lying Leg Curls, Seated Leg Curl and Nordic Curl Negative.

What it does

Starts on the thigh bone itself, below the long head, so it only bends the knee and doesn’t move the hip. Leg curls train it.

Best exercises for the biceps femoris (short head)
