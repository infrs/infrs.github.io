Biceps femoris (long head): what it does and how to train it

Outer hamstring. The best exercises for it include Romanian Deadlift, Good Morning and Stiff-Legged Dumbbell Deadlift.

What it does

The outer hamstring, from the sit bone to the outside of the knee. It extends the hip and bends the knee, and is the hamstring most often strained in sprinting. Hip hinges like Romanian deadlifts work it hard.

Best exercises for the biceps femoris (long head)

  1. Romanian DeadliftBarbell · intermediate · compound
  2. Good MorningBarbell · intermediate · compound
  3. Stiff-Legged Dumbbell DeadliftDumbbell · beginner · compound
