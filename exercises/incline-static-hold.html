Incline Static Hold

Equipment
Dumbbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Especially targets

  • Upper pectoralis majorClavicular head, just under the collarbone

Main muscles

  • Chest

Also works

  • Shoulders
  • Triceps

How to do it

  1. Hold Position: Lower the dumbbells a few inches above your chest — about the midpoint of a normal incline press.
  2. This is where your upper chest is under maximum tension.
  3. Static Hold: Hold the position for 20–45 seconds while keeping the chest tight.
  4. Breathe slowly but stay tense — do not relax your chest or shoulders.
  5. End the set: After the hold, carefully bring the dumbbells down and rest.
