Best traps exercises

Barbell Shrug, Dumbbell Shrug and Farmer’s Carry are among the best exercises for the traps. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the traps.

What the traps muscles do

The trapezius is a large diamond-shaped muscle from the base of the skull down to the middle of the back. Its upper part runs from the neck to the shoulder and lifts the shoulders, as in a shrug or when you carry heavy bags.
