Triceps brachii (lateral head): what it does and how to train it

Outer back of the arm, the "horseshoe". The best exercises for it include Triceps Pushdown, Close-Grip Barbell Bench Press and Dips - Triceps Version.

What it does

On the outer back of the arm, forming the visible horseshoe. It’s strong in heavy pushes like pushdowns, dips and close-grip presses.

Best exercises for the triceps brachii (lateral head)

  1. Triceps PushdownCable · beginner · isolation
  2. Close-Grip Barbell Bench PressBarbell · beginner · compound
  3. Dips - Triceps VersionOther · intermediate · compound
  4. Tricep Dumbbell KickbackDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  5. Bench DipsNo equipment · beginner · compound
  6. Body Tricep PressNo equipment · beginner · isolation
