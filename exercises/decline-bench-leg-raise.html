Decline Bench Leg Raise

Equipment
Other
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Especially targets

  • Lower rectus abdominisThe six-pack below the navel
  • IliopsoasPsoas and iliacus, deep in the belly and the front of the hip; lifts the thigh

Main muscles

  • Abs

Also works

  • Quads

How to do it

  1. Setup : Lie on a decline bench with your head at the top and grip the handles.
  2. The Start : Extend legs fully, keeping them slightly elevated to engage your core.
  3. The Lift : Raise your legs toward the ceiling using your abs, not momentum.
  4. The Squeeze : Pause and contract your lower abdominals at the top.
  5. The Return : Lower your legs slowly to the starting position with control.
