Foods high in iodine

Needed to make thyroid hormones, which set how fast your body uses energy. The richest common sources per serving are kelp, wakame and cod (cooked). The Daily Value is 150 µg a day for adults.

What iodine does

Your thyroid gland uses iodine to make the hormones that control your metabolism, and that a baby’s brain and bones need to grow. How much a food has depends a lot on the soil, the sea and the animal feed it came from.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 150 µg a day for adults. The safe upper limit for adults is 1100 µg a day.

Tips

  • Fish, seaweed, dairy and eggs are the main sources, and iodized salt if you use it.
  • Sea salt, kosher salt and most salt in packaged food aren’t iodized.
  • Seaweed, kelp especially, can have very large amounts: a little goes a long way.

Good to know

  • The adult upper limit is 1,100 µg a day. Too much, like too little, can upset the thyroid.
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding raise the need to 220–290 µg a day.
