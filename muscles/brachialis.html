Brachialis: what it does and how to train it

Under the biceps; makes the upper arm look thicker. The best exercises for it include Hammer Curls, Reverse Barbell Curl and Cross Body Hammer Curl.

What it does

Lies under the biceps and is the elbow’s main bender, whichever way the palm faces. Building it pushes the biceps up and makes the arm look thicker; hammer and reverse curls work it well.

Best exercises for the brachialis

  1. Hammer CurlsDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  2. Reverse Barbell CurlBarbell · beginner · isolation
  3. Cross Body Hammer CurlDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  4. Alternate Hammer CurlDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  5. Cable Hammer Curls - Rope AttachmentCable · beginner · isolation
  6. Incline Hammer CurlsDumbbell · beginner · isolation
