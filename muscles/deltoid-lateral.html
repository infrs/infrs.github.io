Lateral deltoid: what it does and how to train it

Side of the shoulder. The best exercises for it include Side Lateral Raise, Cable Seated Lateral Raise and One-Arm Incline Lateral Raise.

What it does

The side of the shoulder, which gives it its width. It lifts the arm out to the side, as in lateral raises, and is hard to train with presses alone.

Best exercises for the lateral deltoid

  1. Side Lateral RaiseDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  2. Cable Seated Lateral RaiseCable · beginner · isolation
  3. One-Arm Incline Lateral RaiseDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  4. Upright Barbell RowBarbell · beginner · compound
  5. Alternating Deltoid RaiseDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  6. Dumbbell ScaptionDumbbell · beginner · isolation
