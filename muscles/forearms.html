Best forearms & hands exercises

Farmer’s Carry, Palms-Up Barbell Wrist Curl Over A Bench and Palms-Down Wrist Curl Over A Bench are among the best exercises for the forearms & hands. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the forearms & hands.

What the forearms & hands muscles do

About twenty muscles run from the elbow to the wrist and fingers, with more small muscles in the hand itself. Those on the palm side bend the wrist and fingers to grip; those on the back straighten them. A strong grip helps almost every pulling and carrying exercise.

Muscles of the forearms & hands

  • BrachioradialisTop of the forearm; bends the elbow
  • Pronator teresTurns the palm down
  • Flexor carpi radialisBends the wrist, thumb side
  • Palmaris longusMiddle of the inner forearm (some people don’t have it)
  • Flexor carpi ulnarisBends the wrist, little-finger side
  • Flexor digitorum superficialisBends the fingers; key for grip
  • Extensor carpi radialis longusStraightens the wrist, thumb side
  • Extensor carpi radialis brevisStraightens the wrist
  • Extensor digitorumStraightens the fingers
  • Extensor digiti minimiStraightens the little finger
  • Extensor carpi ulnarisStraightens the wrist, little-finger side
  • Abductor pollicis longusMoves the thumb away from the hand
  • Extensor pollicis brevisStraightens the thumb
  • Thenar musclesThe ball of the thumb
  • Hypothenar musclesThe little-finger side of the palm
  • Lumbricals and interosseiSmall muscles between the bones of the hand
  • Flexor digitorum profundusDeep finger flexor, under the superficial one; bends the fingertips
  • SupinatorDeep in the upper forearm; turns the palm up
