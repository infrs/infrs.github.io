How to do it

  1. Lie face down with your arms straight overhead and your legs together.
  2. Squeeze your glutes and lift your arms, chest and legs off the floor, so only your hips and lower stomach touch it.
  3. Keep your arms by your ears and your neck long, looking at the floor.
  4. Hold, breathing steadily, then lower.

It’s the opposite shape of the hollow body hold. Training both builds the body tension that every calisthenics skill needs.
