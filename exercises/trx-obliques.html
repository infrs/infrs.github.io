TRX Obliques

Equipment
Other
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Especially targets

  • External obliqueSides of the waist
  • Internal obliqueUnder the external oblique; twists and bends the trunk

Main muscles

  • Abs
  • Chest

Also works

  • Shoulders

How to do it

  1. Place your feet in the stirrups and assume a high plank with your hands directly beneath your shoulders.
  2. Pull your knees to your right elbow, then push them back out and to the centre.
