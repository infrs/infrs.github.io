Bent-Over Lateral Raises

Equipment
Dumbbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Especially targets

  • Lateral deltoidSide of the shoulder
  • Posterior deltoidBack of the shoulder

Main muscles

  • Shoulders

Also works

  • Middle back

How to do it

  1. Sit on bench while holding weights. Bend forward as far as possible, with arms slightly bent at the elbow.
  2. Perform a lateral raise while maintaining the bend in your elbow.
