Middle pectoralis major: what it does and how to train it

Sternocostal head, the bulk of the chest. The best exercises for it include Barbell Bench Press - Medium Grip, Dumbbell Bench Press and Dumbbell Flyes.

What it does

The largest part of the chest, from the breastbone and upper ribs to the upper arm. It does most of the work in flat presses, push-ups and flyes, pulling the arm across the body.

Best exercises for the middle pectoralis major

  1. Barbell Bench Press - Medium GripBarbell · beginner · compound
  2. Dumbbell Bench PressDumbbell · beginner · compound
  3. Dumbbell FlyesDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  4. PushupsNo equipment · beginner · compound
  5. ButterflyMachine · beginner · isolation
  6. Alternating Floor PressKettlebells · beginner · compound
