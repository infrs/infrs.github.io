Finger Pushup

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

  • Middle pectoralis majorSternocostal head, the bulk of the chest

Main muscles

  • Chest

Also works

  • Shoulders
  • Abs
  • Triceps

How to do it

  1. Hand Positioning:
  2. Start in a plank position with your hands directly beneath your shoulders.
  3. Spread your fingers wide apart, placing them firmly on the ground.
  4. Finger Placement:
  5. Gradually lift your palms off the ground, shifting the weight onto your fingers.
  6. Focus on distributing the load evenly across your fingertips and thumbs.
  7. Body Alignment:
  8. Maintain a straight line from head to heels to engage your core.
  9. Keep your body in a controlled and stable position throughout the exercise.
  10. Lowering Phase:
  11. Slowly bend your elbows, lowering your chest towards the ground.
  12. Ensure controlled movement, maintaining stability on your fingertips.
  13. Pushing Up:
  14. Press through your fingertips to straighten your arms, returning to the starting position.
  15. Emphasize the engagement of your fingers and thumbs throughout the push-up.
