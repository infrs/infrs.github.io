Hanging Knee Raise

Equipment
Other
Level
Beginner
Type
Strength
Movement
Isolation (one joint)
Force
Pull

Especially targets

  • Lower rectus abdominisThe six-pack below the navel
  • IliopsoasPsoas and iliacus, deep in the belly and the front of the hip; lifts the thigh

Main muscles

  • Abs

Also works

  • Forearms & hands

How to do it

  1. Hang from a pull-up bar with straight arms.
  2. Without swinging, bring your knees up toward your chest, curling your pelvis up at the top.
  3. Pause.
  4. Lower your legs slowly until they are straight.
