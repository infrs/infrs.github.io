Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row

Equipment
Dumbbell
Level
Beginner
Type
Strength
Movement
Compound (several joints)
Force
Pull

Especially targets

  • Middle trapeziusTransverse part, across the shoulder blades
  • Rhomboid majorBetween the shoulder blades, under the trapezius
  • Rhomboid minorAbove the rhomboid major, under the trapezius

Main muscles

  • Middle back

Also works

  • Lats
  • Biceps
  • Shoulders

How to do it

  1. Set a bench to a 30–45° incline and lie face down on it with a dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Let the dumbbells hang straight down under your shoulders.
  3. Row the dumbbells up toward your hips, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
  4. Lower them under control.

The bench takes your lower back out of it, so you can focus on your upper back.
