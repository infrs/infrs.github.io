How to do it

  1. Lie on a bench and grab its edge (or something solid) behind your head.
  2. Swing your legs and hips up so your weight rests on your upper back and your body is straight.
  3. Lower your body as one straight line as slowly as you can, without bending at the hips.
  4. Bend your knees at the bottom, bring them back up and repeat.
