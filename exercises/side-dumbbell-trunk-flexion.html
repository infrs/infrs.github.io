Side Dumbbell Trunk Flexion

Equipment
Dumbbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Main muscles

  • Abs

How to do it

  1. AKA dumbbell side bends.
  2. Stand in line with the hips with slightly bent knees, maintain the natural curvature of the spine, hand stretched by the body, grip the barbell with one hand.
  3. Make slow and controlled torso side flexions till you reach the angle of approximately 45°.
