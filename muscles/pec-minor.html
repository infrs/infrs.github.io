Pectoralis minor: what it does and how to train it

Under the pectoralis major; pulls the shoulder blade forward and down. The best exercises for it include Barbell Bench Press - Medium Grip, Incline Dumbbell Press and Dips - Chest Version.

What it does

A small, thin muscle under the pectoralis major, from the upper ribs to the front of the shoulder blade. It pulls the shoulder blade forward and down; when it’s tight from long hours of sitting, it helps round the shoulders forward.

Best exercises for the pectoralis minor

  1. Barbell Bench Press - Medium GripBarbell · beginner · compound
  2. Incline Dumbbell PressDumbbell · beginner · compound
  3. Dips - Chest VersionOther · intermediate · compound
  4. Dumbbell Bench PressDumbbell · beginner · compound
  5. Cable CrossoverCable · beginner · isolation
  6. PushupsNo equipment · beginner · compound
