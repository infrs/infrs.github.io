Biceps brachii (long head): what it does and how to train it

Outer head of the biceps. The best exercises for it include Incline Dumbbell Curl, Bayesian Cable Curl and Barbell Curl.

What it does

The outer head of the biceps, whose tendon runs over the top of the shoulder joint. Curls with the arms behind the body, like incline curls, stretch it and work it most.

Best exercises for the biceps brachii (long head)
