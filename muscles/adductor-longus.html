Adductor longus: what it does and how to train it

Front of the inner thigh. The best exercises for it include Copenhagen Plank, Thigh Adductor and Cossack Squat.

What it does

The adductor at the front of the inner thigh, and the one most often strained in a groin pull. It pulls the thigh in toward the other leg.

Best exercises for the adductor longus

  1. Copenhagen PlankNo equipment · expert · compound
  2. Thigh AdductorMachine · beginner · isolation
  3. Cossack SquatNo equipment · intermediate · compound
  4. Band Hip AdductionsBands · beginner · isolation
  5. Side-Lying Hip AdductionNo equipment · beginner · isolation
  6. Short-Lever Copenhagen PlankNo equipment · intermediate · compound
