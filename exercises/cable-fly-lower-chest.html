Cable Fly Lower Chest

Equipment
Cable
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Main muscles

  • Chest

Also works

  • Shoulders
  • Biceps

How to do it

  1. Start position: Arms extended out to your sides in a “Y” shape.
  2. Feel a stretch in your chest (slight bend in elbows — don’t lock them).
  3. Movement: Bring your hands downward and together in front of your lower chest or upper abs, in a smooth arc motion.
  4. Squeeze your chest hard at the bottom for 1–2 seconds.
  5. Return: Slowly raise your arms back up along the same path until you feel a good stretch in your chest.
  6. Maintain control — don’t let the weights pull you back too quickly.
