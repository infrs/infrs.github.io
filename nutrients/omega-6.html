What omega-6 (linoleic acid) does

Linoleic acid is the main omega-6 fat. Your body needs it for healthy skin and cell membranes and can’t make it. When it replaces saturated fat, it helps lower LDL cholesterol.

How much you need

About 12–17 g

Tips

  • Most diets get plenty from vegetable oils, nuts and seeds.
  • You don’t need to avoid omega-6. Just make sure you also get enough omega-3.

Good to know

  • Adults need about 12 g (women) to 17 g (men) a day.
  • Sunflower, corn and soybean oils, walnuts and pine nuts are among the richest sources.
