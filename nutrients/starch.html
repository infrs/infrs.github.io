Foods high in starch

The main carbohydrate in grains, potatoes, beans and bananas, and a steady source of energy. The richest common sources per serving are soft pretzel, fufu and white rice (cooked, fortified).

What starch does

Starch is how plants store energy: long chains of glucose that your gut breaks back down into glucose. Whole grains, beans and potatoes with their skins bring it with fiber, so it’s absorbed more slowly than sugar. Some starch, called resistant starch, escapes digestion and feeds your gut bacteria like fiber does.

How much you need

No set amount: it’s most of the carbohydrate in grains, potatoes and beans

Tips

  • Cooked and cooled rice, potatoes and pasta have more resistant starch, which raises blood sugar less.
  • Before and after training, starchy foods refill the glycogen your muscles burn.

Good to know

  • USDA measures starch for only some foods; for the others the app works it out as the carbohydrates minus the sugars and fiber.
