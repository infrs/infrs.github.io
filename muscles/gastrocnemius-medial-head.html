Gastrocnemius (medial head): what it does and how to train it

Inner upper calf; works most with straight knees. The best exercises for it include Standing Calf Raises, Single-Leg Calf Raise and Donkey Calf Raises.

What it does

The larger, inner head of the two-headed calf muscle. It crosses both the knee and the ankle, so it points the foot hardest with the knee straight, as in standing calf raises.
