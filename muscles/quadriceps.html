Best quads exercises

Barbell Squat, Front Squat (Clean Grip) and Bulgarian Split Squat are among the best exercises for the quads. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the quads.

What the quads muscles do

The quadriceps on the front of the thigh straighten the knee; the rectus femoris, the only one that crosses the hip, also lifts the thigh. The long, thin sartorius crosses over them. They’re key for standing up, climbing stairs and jumping.
