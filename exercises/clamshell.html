How to do it

  1. Lie on your side with your hips and knees bent and your feet together.
  2. Keeping your feet touching, lift your top knee as high as you can without rolling your hips back.
  3. Pause at the top.
  4. Lower slowly. Finish all reps, then switch sides.

Loop a resistance band just above your knees to make it harder.
