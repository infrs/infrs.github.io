Main muscles

How to do it

  1. Hold a hand gripper in one hand, its handles across your palm and fingers, your arm relaxed at your side or bent in front of you.
  2. Squeeze it closed as far as you can, the handles touching if you can.
  3. Open your hand slowly against the spring.
  4. Do all the reps, then switch hands.

Half or partial closes count while you are building up to a stronger gripper.
