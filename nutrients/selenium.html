Foods high in selenium

Supports antioxidant enzymes, the thyroid and immunity. The richest common sources per serving are brazil nuts, yellowfin tuna (cooked) and mussels (cooked). The Daily Value is 55 µg a day for adults.

What selenium does

Selenium is part of enzymes that protect cells from damage and that make and activate thyroid hormones. It also supports your immune system.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 55 µg a day for adults. The safe upper limit for adults is 400 µg a day.

Tips

  • Brazil nuts are so rich that one or two a day is plenty. Don’t overdo them.
  • Seafood, meat, eggs and whole grains are reliable everyday sources.

Good to know

  • How much selenium plant foods contain depends on the soil they grew in, which varies a lot between regions.
  • Getting more than the 400 µg upper limit for a long time can make hair and nails brittle and give your breath a garlic smell.
