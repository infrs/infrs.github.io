Glute Bridge Single-Arm Press

Equipment
Dumbbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Main muscles

  • Chest

Also works

  • Shoulders
  • Biceps
  • Hamstrings
  • Glutes
  • Triceps

How to do it

  1. With one dumbbell in hand, perform a glute bridge. Now hold the dumbbell above your chest, this is the rep starting position.
  2. Lower the dumbbell so that your elbow touches the floor or is roughly 45 degrees below your shoulder if using a bench.
  3. Push the dumbbell back into the starting position.
