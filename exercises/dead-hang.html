How to do it

  1. Grab a pull-up bar with an overhand grip, hands a little wider than your shoulders.
  2. Lift your feet or bend your knees so you hang with straight arms.
  3. Relax your shoulders up toward your ears and breathe.
  4. Hold for time, then step down.

Builds grip strength and gets your shoulders used to hanging before pull-ups.
