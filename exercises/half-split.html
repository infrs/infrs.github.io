How to do it

  1. Kneel on one knee and straighten your front leg out in front of you, heel on the floor and toes pointing up.
  2. Put your hands on the floor, or on books or blocks, on either side of your front leg.
  3. Keep your back long and hinge forward from your hips until you feel a stretch along the back of the front thigh.
  4. Hold, breathing slowly, then switch legs.

Hinge from your hips rather than rounding your back: aim your belly toward your thigh.
