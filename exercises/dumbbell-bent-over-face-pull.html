Dumbbell Bent Over Face Pull

Equipment
Dumbbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Especially targets

  • Posterior deltoidBack of the shoulder
  • Middle trapeziusTransverse part, across the shoulder blades
  • Rhomboid majorBetween the shoulder blades, under the trapezius
  • Rhomboid minorAbove the rhomboid major, under the trapezius

Main muscles

  • Shoulders
  • Lats

Also works

  • Traps

How to do it

  1. This exercise involves using dumbbells to perform a bent over face pull, which targets the upper back and shoulders.
  2. The movement involves pulling the weights towards the face while keeping the elbows high and squeezing the shoulder blades together.
