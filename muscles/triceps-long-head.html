Triceps brachii (long head): what it does and how to train it

Inner back of the arm; the biggest head. The best exercises for it include Cable Rope Overhead Triceps Extension, Standing Overhead Barbell Triceps Extension and Lying Triceps Press.

What it does

The biggest head, on the inner back of the arm. It’s the only head that crosses the shoulder, so overhead extensions, which stretch it, work it most.

Best exercises for the triceps brachii (long head)

  2. Standing Overhead Barbell Triceps ExtensionBarbell · beginner · isolation
  3. Lying Triceps PressBarbell · intermediate · isolation
