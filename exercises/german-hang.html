How to do it

  1. Do a skin the cat and stop at the bottom, hanging behind your arms with your hips low and feet pointing to the floor.
  2. Let your shoulders open and relax into the stretch, breathing slowly.
  3. If you can, rest your feet lightly on the floor to take some of your weight.
  4. Come out by pulling your knees back through your arms.

Start with 10–20 seconds, and only as deep as feels like a stretch, never pain.
