Cossack Squat

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength
Movement
Compound (several joints)
Force
Push

Main muscles

  • Inner thighs

Also works

  • Quads
  • Glutes
  • Hamstrings

How to do it

  1. Stand with your feet about twice shoulder width apart, toes turned slightly out.
  2. Shift your weight to one side and squat down on that leg, keeping the other leg straight with its toes pointing up.
  3. Go as low as you can while keeping your heel down and chest up.
  4. Push back up to the middle and repeat on the other side.
