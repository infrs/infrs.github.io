Main muscles

Also works

  • Glutes

How to do it

  1. Sit tall and bring the soles of your feet together, letting your knees fall out to the sides.
  2. Hold your feet and draw your heels as close to your body as is comfortable.
  3. Keep your back long and lean forward gently from your hips.
  4. Hold, breathing slowly. You can press your knees down gently with your elbows.
