Foods high in fluoride

Hardens tooth enamel and helps protect teeth from decay. The richest common sources per serving are black tea (brewed), karak tea and grape juice.

What fluoride does

Fluoride becomes part of your teeth and bones and makes tooth enamel harder for acids to wear away. Most comes from fluoridated tap water, tea and toothpaste rather than food.

How much you need

About 3 mg a day for women and 4 mg for men The safe upper limit for adults is 10 mg a day.

Tips

  • Tea is one of the richest food sources.
  • Fluoride toothpaste works best spat out, not rinsed away, so some stays on your teeth.

Good to know

  • The adult upper limit is 10 mg a day. In children, too much while teeth are forming can leave white marks on them.
  • USDA has fluoride for only some foods, mostly drinks, so a day’s total here is a minimum.
