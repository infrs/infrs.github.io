Foods high in zinc

Supports immunity, wound healing, growth, taste and smell. The richest common sources per serving are oysters (cooked), beef short ribs (braised) and beef stew meat (cooked). The Daily Value is 11 mg a day for adults.

What zinc does

Zinc is needed by hundreds of enzymes. It helps your immune system fight infections, heals wounds, supports growth during childhood and pregnancy, and is needed for normal taste and smell.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 11 mg a day for adults. The safe upper limit for adults is 40 mg a day.

Tips

  • Oysters are by far the richest source; red meat, crab, beans, seeds and nuts also help.
  • Zinc from animal foods is absorbed better. Soaking, sprouting or fermenting beans and grains (as in sourdough bread) helps with plant sources.

What blocks it

  • Bran and unsoaked beans or grains: Their phytates bind zinc. Soaking, sprouting and sourdough fermenting free more of it.
  • Iron supplements: Large doses of iron taken at the same time compete with zinc to be absorbed.

Good to know

  • Your body has no special zinc store, so you need some every day.
  • Getting more than 40 mg a day for a long time, usually from supplements, can cause copper deficiency.
