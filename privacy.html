Privacy Policy

Body Atlas: Train & Nutrition · Effective 10 November 2026

This policy explains what the app Body Atlas: Train & Nutrition ("the app"), published by infrs ("I", "me"), does with information. The short version: I run no servers and have no user accounts, so I don't collect or store your personal information. Your data stays on your phone, apart from what Google's ad, payment, rating and update services handle, and any email you choose to send me, described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so it can remember it between visits. It is never sent to me or to any server I control.

  • Exercises and foods you save.
  • Your chosen language, colour theme, background, app icon and display settings.
  • Profile and macro calculator details, if you enter them: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goal, target weight and pace, your own calorie, macro and nutrient targets, and preferred units.
  • Your journal, if you use it: workouts (exercises, sets, weights, reps, time), cardio sessions (activity, intensity, minutes and speed), the foods and water you log, body weight and notes, kept by date.
  • Your training plan and the answers you gave to make it (goal, experience, days, equipment, focus areas and areas to go easy on), plus your own foods and saved meals.
  • Reminder settings: which workout and water reminders you turned on and when.
  • The cards you have collected, the date of your last free card and how many rewarded videos you have watched.
  • Which in-app tours you have already seen.
  • When you first opened the app, how many times you have opened it, and whether you have already been asked for a rating, so the rating request appears at most once.
  • Your supporter status: which badges you own, tip totals and Google Play order IDs, so a purchase is credited once.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes all of this.

Backup files

The app can export your journal, plan, own foods and saved meals to a backup file. When you export, the app hands the file to Android's share sheet and you choose where it goes (for example your files, a cloud drive or a message to yourself). When you import, the app reads only the one file you pick, to restore your data. I never receive these files. Anyone you give a backup file to can read what is in it.

Notifications

If you turn on workout or water reminders, the app asks Android for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to a server. The "Add" button on a water reminder records the water in your journal on the phone, even when the app is closed. You can turn reminders off in the app or block notifications in Android settings at any time.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded video ads that unlock colour themes and give a collectible card. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. I do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and no ads load unless consent allows them. Where Google requires it, an Ad privacy settings button in the app's About & credits screen lets you review or change your choice at any time. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

More: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers optional one-time purchases: supporter badges and tips. They are processed entirely by Google Play. I never see or store your payment card or billing details. The app receives only the purchase result, such as the product and order ID, so it can unlock your badge and restore it if you reinstall the app. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles payments.

Ratings and app updates (Google Play)

Once, after you have used the app for a few days, it may ask Google Play to show its rating window. Google decides whether to show it, and any rating or review you leave goes to Google Play, not to me, under the Google Privacy Policy. The app also asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, so it can offer to update. These checks go between your phone and Google Play; I receive nothing from them.

Emailing me

The app has a Contact link, and Diamond supporters can send me a name for the app's credits. Both open your own email app with a message to me. Nothing is sent until you press send. The credits message is filled in with the name you typed, which supporter badge or tips you bought, and your Google Play order numbers, so I can check the purchase. If you send an email, I receive your email address and what you wrote. I use it only to reply, and for credits, to check the order and add the name you chose to the app. I keep these emails in my inbox and don't share them. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the app hands text and a link to Android's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The same happens when you export a backup file. Nothing is shared unless you do it.

What I don't do

  • I don't require an account or sign-in.
  • I don't use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools of my own.
  • I don't access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The app opens a file only when you pick a backup file to import.
  • I don't sell your information.

The only services the app contacts over the internet are Google AdMob and Google Play (purchases, ratings and updates).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Health information

The app provides general fitness and nutrition information and estimates. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, dietitian or qualified trainer. Details you enter in the macro calculator, your journal, your plan and your body weight stay on your device unless you export a backup file yourself.

Your choices and rights

I hold no personal information about you, unless you email me. In that case, you can ask me to show you or delete your emails by writing to the address below. You can delete everything the app stores by clearing its data or uninstalling it. Backup files you exported are not deleted with the app; delete them wherever you saved them. For data held by Google (ads and payments), use your Google Account controls at myaccount.google.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, I will update this page and the effective date above. Please check back from time to time.
