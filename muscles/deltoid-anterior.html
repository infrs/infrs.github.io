Anterior deltoid: what it does and how to train it

Front of the shoulder. The best exercises for it include Barbell Shoulder Press, Dumbbell Shoulder Press and Arnold Dumbbell Press.

What it does

The front of the shoulder. It raises the arm forward and helps in every press and push, so it often gets plenty of work from chest training already.

Best exercises for the anterior deltoid

  1. Barbell Shoulder PressBarbell · intermediate · compound
  2. Dumbbell Shoulder PressDumbbell · intermediate · compound
  3. Arnold Dumbbell PressDumbbell · intermediate · compound
  4. Landmine PressBarbell · beginner · compound
  5. Alternating Cable Shoulder PressCable · beginner · compound
  6. Anti-Gravity PressBarbell · beginner · compound
