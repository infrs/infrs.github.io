How to do it

  1. Start on your hands and knees, hands under shoulders and knees under hips.
  2. Brace your core so your back stays flat.
  3. Reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back until both are in line with your body.
  4. Pause, return, and switch sides without letting your hips tip.
