Straight Bar Dip

Equipment
Other
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength
Movement
Compound (several joints)
Force
Push

Especially targets

  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)Outer back of the arm, the "horseshoe"

Main muscles

  • Triceps

Also works

  • Chest
  • Shoulders

How to do it

  1. Jump to a support on top of a straight bar: arms straight, bar at your hips, hands about shoulder width apart.
  2. Lean your chest slightly forward over the bar and bend your elbows, lowering until the bar is near your upper stomach.
  3. Push back up until your arms are straight.
  4. Keep your legs slightly in front of you for balance.

This is the top half of a muscle-up. Get comfortable with sets of 10 before trying one.
