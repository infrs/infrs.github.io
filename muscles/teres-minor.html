Teres minor: what it does and how to train it

Rotator cuff, along the outer edge of the shoulder blade. The best exercises for it include External Rotation with Cable, External Rotation and Face Pull.

What it does

A small rotator cuff muscle along the outer edge of the shoulder blade. It helps the infraspinatus turn the arm outward and steadies the shoulder joint.

Best exercises for the teres minor

  1. External Rotation with CableCable · beginner · isolation
  2. External RotationDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  3. Face PullCable · intermediate · compound
  4. Car DriversBarbell · beginner · isolation
  5. External Rotation with BandBands · beginner · compound
  6. Cuban PressDumbbell · intermediate · compound
