Foods high in magnesium

Involved in muscles, nerves, blood sugar, blood pressure and energy. The richest common sources per serving are hemp seeds, pumpkin seeds and watermelon seeds. The Daily Value is 420 mg a day for adults.

What magnesium does

Magnesium helps more than 300 enzymes, including ones that make energy, build protein, and control blood sugar and blood pressure. It keeps your heart rhythm steady and helps muscles relax after they contract.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 420 mg a day for adults. The safe upper limit for adults is 350 mg a day from supplements and fortified foods.

Tips

  • Nuts, seeds, legumes, whole grains and leafy greens are top sources. Pumpkin and hemp seeds are among the richest.
  • Refining grains removes most of their magnesium, so choose whole grains.

What blocks it

  • High-dose zinc supplements: Very large doses of zinc, well above 100 mg a day, get in the way of magnesium absorption.

Good to know

  • Many people get less than recommended from food.
  • High-dose supplements can cause diarrhea. The upper limit from supplements is 350 mg a day; food doesn’t count toward it.
