Scalenes: what it does and how to train it

Side of the neck (anterior, middle and posterior scalene). The best exercises for it include Isometric Neck Exercise - Sides, Side Neck Stretch and Isometric Neck Exercise - Front And Back.

What it does

Three muscles on each side of the neck, from the neck vertebrae to the top ribs. They tilt the neck to the side and lift the top ribs when you breathe hard.

Best exercises for the scalenes
