Pendlay Row

Equipment
Barbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength
Movement
Compound (several joints)
Force
Pull

Especially targets

  • Middle trapeziusTransverse part, across the shoulder blades
  • Rhomboid majorBetween the shoulder blades, under the trapezius
  • Rhomboid minorAbove the rhomboid major, under the trapezius

Main muscles

  • Middle back

Also works

  • Lats
  • Biceps
  • Lower back

How to do it

  1. Stand with the barbell over your mid-foot. Hinge at the hips until your back is about parallel to the floor and grab the bar a little wider than shoulder width.
  2. Brace your core and keep your back flat.
  3. Pull the bar explosively to your lower chest.
  4. Lower it all the way back to the floor and reset before the next rep.
