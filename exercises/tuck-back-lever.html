How to do it

  1. From a German hang, pull your knees tight to your chest.
  2. Press down through straight arms and raise your hips until your back is level with the floor, facing down.
  3. Keep your arms straight and your body tight, and hold.
  4. Come out the way you went in, or tuck through to a hang.

It puts a big stretch on the biceps and the front of the shoulders: warm up well and build up gradually.
