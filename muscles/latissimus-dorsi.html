Latissimus dorsi: what it does and how to train it

The wide muscle that shapes the back. The best exercises for it include Pullups, Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown and One-Arm Dumbbell Row.

What it does

The widest muscle of the back, from the middle and lower spine and the top of the pelvis up to the upper arm. It pulls the arms down and back, as in pull-ups, pulldowns and rows, and powers swimming strokes.
