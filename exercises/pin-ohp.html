Pin OHP

Equipment
Barbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Main muscles

  • Shoulders
  • Triceps

Also works

  • Traps

How to do it

  1. Set security pins to the height of your lower chin
  2. Rest bar on pins keeping tension
  3. Raise bar over head
