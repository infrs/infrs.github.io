How to do it

  1. Grab a sturdy vertical pole with both hands, a little more than shoulder width apart: one hand above your head, the other at about hip height.
  2. Push hard with your bottom arm and pull with your top arm, keeping both straight.
  3. Jump your hips up and pull your knees to your chest so your body is held sideways off the floor.
  4. Hold, then lower your feet with control. Train both sides.
