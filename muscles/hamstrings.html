Best hamstrings exercises

Romanian Deadlift, Lying Leg Curls and Seated Leg Curl are among the best exercises for the hamstrings. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the hamstrings.

What the hamstrings muscles do

Three muscles on the back of the thigh, one of them with two heads, bend the knee and, all but the short head, extend the hip. They’re easily strained when sprinting, so training them helps protect the knees and hips.

Muscles of the hamstrings

  • Biceps femoris (long head)Outer hamstring
  • Biceps femoris (short head)Outer hamstring, lower part; only bends the knee
  • SemitendinosusInner hamstring
  • SemimembranosusInner hamstring, under the semitendinosus
