Best lats exercises

Pullups, Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown and One-Arm Dumbbell Row are among the best exercises for the lats. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the lats.

What the lats muscles do

The latissimus dorsi is the widest muscle of the back, running from the lower spine and pelvis up to the upper arm, with the teres major beside it. They pull the arms down and back, as in pull-ups and rows, and give the back its V shape.
