Sartorius: what it does and how to train it

Long strap across the front of the thigh. The best exercises for it include Barbell Squat, Front Squat (Clean Grip) and Bulgarian Split Squat.

What it does

The longest muscle in the body, a thin strap crossing the thigh from the outer hip to the inner knee. It helps bend, lift and turn out the leg, as when you sit cross-legged.

Best exercises for the sartorius

  1. Barbell SquatBarbell · beginner · compound
  2. Front Squat (Clean Grip)Barbell · intermediate · compound
  3. Bulgarian Split SquatNo equipment · intermediate · compound
  4. Leg PressMachine · beginner · compound
  5. Hack SquatMachine · beginner · compound
  6. Leg ExtensionsMachine · beginner · isolation
