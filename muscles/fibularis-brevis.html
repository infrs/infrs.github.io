Fibularis brevis: what it does and how to train it

Outer lower leg, near the ankle. The best exercises for it include Standing Calf Raises, Seated Calf Raise and Single-Leg Calf Raise.

What it does

Shorter, lying under the longus near the ankle. It turns the foot outward and helps protect the ankle from sprains.
