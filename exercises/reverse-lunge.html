Reverse Lunge

Equipment
No equipment

Main muscles

Also works

  • Glutes
  • Hamstrings

How to do it

  1. Stand tall with your feet hip width apart.
  2. Take a long step back with one foot and lower your back knee toward the floor.
  3. Stop when your front thigh is about parallel to the floor, front knee over your mid-foot.
  4. Push through your front foot to step back to standing. Alternate legs.

Easier on the knees than a forward lunge, so it is a good place to start.
