Privacy Policy

Body Atlas: training and nutrition · Effective 16 October 2026

This policy explains what the app Body Atlas: training and nutrition ("the app"), published by infrs ("I", "me"), does with information. The short version: I run no servers and have no user accounts, so I don't collect or store your personal information. Your data stays on your phone, apart from what Google's ad and payment services handle, described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so it can remember it between visits. It is never sent to me or to any server I control.

  • Exercises and foods you save.
  • Your chosen language, colour theme and display settings.
  • Macro calculator details, if you use it: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goal and preferred units.
  • Your supporter status: which badges you own, tip totals and Google Play order IDs, so a purchase is credited once.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes all of this.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded video ads that unlock colour themes. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. I do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and no ads load unless consent allows them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

More: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers optional one-time purchases: supporter badges and tips. They are processed entirely by Google Play. I never see or store your payment card or billing details. The app receives only the purchase result, such as the product and order ID, so it can unlock your badge and restore it if you reinstall the app. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles payments.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the app hands text and a link to Android's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is shared unless you do it.

What I don't do

  • I don't require an account or sign-in.
  • I don't use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools of my own.
  • I don't collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • I don't sell your information.

The only services the app contacts over the internet are Google AdMob and Google Play.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Health information

The app provides general fitness and nutrition information and estimates. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, dietitian or qualified trainer. Details you enter in the macro calculator stay on your device.

Your choices and rights

Because I hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete on my side. You can delete everything the app stores by clearing its data or uninstalling it. For data held by Google (ads and payments), use your Google Account controls at myaccount.google.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, I will update this page and the effective date above. Please check back from time to time.
