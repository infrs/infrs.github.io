Foods high in vitamin B6 (pyridoxine)

Needed to use protein, make brain messengers and keep immunity strong. The richest common sources per serving are energy drink, yellowfin tuna (cooked) and salmon, wild (cooked). The Daily Value is 1.7 mg a day for adults.

What vitamin B6 (pyridoxine) does

Vitamin B6 helps more than 100 enzymes, most of them involved in using protein. It’s needed to make brain messengers such as serotonin and dopamine, to form hemoglobin for your red blood cells, and to keep your immune system working.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 1.7 mg a day for adults. The safe upper limit for adults is 100 mg a day.

Tips

  • Fish, poultry, potatoes, chickpeas and bananas are good sources.
  • It’s water-soluble and partly lost in cooking water, so steam or roast when you can.

Good to know

  • Older adults, and people with kidney disease or who drink heavily, are more likely to run low.
  • Taking more than 100 mg a day from supplements for a long time can damage nerves. Amounts in food are safe.
