How to do it

  1. Stand in a lunge with your arms overhead, then put your hands on the floor shoulder width apart, fingers spread.
  2. Kick up with your back leg and bring the other to meet it, stacking your hips over your shoulders.
  3. Balance with your fingers: press them into the floor if you start to tip forward, and with the heels of your hands if you tip back.
  4. Keep your arms locked, legs together and body straight. To come down, step one foot down or roll out with your chin tucked.

Learn to come down safely first: turn your hips and step one foot down to the side, like a cartwheel.

Short, frequent practice works best: many tries of a few seconds each.
