How to do it

  1. Squat down and put your hands flat on the floor about shoulder width apart, fingers spread.
  2. Bend your elbows slightly and rest your knees on the backs of your upper arms, just above the elbows.
  3. Lean forward, shifting your weight into your hands, until your feet float off the floor.
  4. Look slightly ahead of your hands and hold, then lower your feet with control.

Put a cushion in front of you the first few times, in case you tip forward.
