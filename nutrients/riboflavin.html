Foods high in vitamin B2 (riboflavin)

Helps produce energy and keeps skin, eyes and cells healthy. The richest common sources per serving are beef liver (cooked), vanilla or strawberry milkshake and chocolate milkshake. The Daily Value is 1.3 mg a day for adults.

What vitamin B2 (riboflavin) does

Riboflavin is part of two coenzymes that help turn food into energy. It also helps your body use other B vitamins, such as vitamin B6 and folate, and protects cells from damage.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 1.3 mg a day for adults.

Tips

  • Milk, yogurt, eggs, meat, mushrooms and almonds are good sources.
  • Light breaks it down, which is why milk is best stored in opaque containers.

Good to know

  • Extra riboflavin turns urine bright yellow. It’s harmless.
