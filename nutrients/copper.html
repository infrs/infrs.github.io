Foods high in copper

Helps make red blood cells and supports nerves, immunity and energy. The richest common sources per serving are beef liver (cooked), oysters (cooked) and calamari (fried). The Daily Value is 0.9 mg a day for adults.

What copper does

Copper helps your body use iron to make red blood cells. It’s also needed to produce energy, build connective tissue and blood vessels, and keep your nervous and immune systems working.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 0.9 mg a day for adults. The safe upper limit for adults is 10 mg a day.

Tips

  • Shellfish, nuts, seeds, organ meats, whole grains and dark chocolate are rich sources.
  • High-dose zinc supplements block copper absorption.

What blocks it

  • High-dose zinc supplements: Zinc makes your gut hold on to copper, so less of it reaches your blood.

Good to know

  • Liver is so rich in copper that one serving covers several days’ needs.
