Best neck exercises

Lying Face Up Plate Neck Resistance, Lying Face Down Plate Neck Resistance and Isometric Neck Exercise - Front And Back are among the best exercises for the neck. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the neck.

What the neck muscles do

Several layers of muscle hold up and move the head, which weighs about 5 kg. They turn, tilt and nod it, and the ones at the back and sides keep it upright all day, which is why they stiffen after hours at a screen.
