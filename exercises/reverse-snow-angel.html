Reverse Snow Angel

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Main muscles

  • Traps

Also works

  • Shoulders
  • Hamstrings
  • Glutes
  • Abs

How to do it

  1. Lay flat on your stomach with your arms extended in front of you on the ground as your legs are lying flat.
  2. Lift both your arms and move them to your side slowly. Then, move them back.
