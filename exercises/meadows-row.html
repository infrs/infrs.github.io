Meadows Row

Equipment
Barbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength
Movement
Compound (several joints)
Force
Pull

Main muscles

  • Lats

Also works

  • Middle back
  • Biceps
  • Shoulders

How to do it

  1. Wedge one end of a barbell into a corner or landmine and load the other end.
  2. Stand side-on to the end of the bar in a staggered stance and hinge forward, resting your free forearm on your front knee.
  3. Grab the end of the bar with an overhand grip and row it toward your hip, elbow flaring out slightly.
  4. Lower under control and switch sides after your set.
