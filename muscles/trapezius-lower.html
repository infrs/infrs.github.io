Lower trapezius: what it does and how to train it

Ascending part, below the shoulder blades. The best exercises for it include Incline Y-Raise, Scapular Pull-Up and Dumbbell Scaption.

What it does

The lower part of the trapezius, angling up from the middle of the back. It pulls the shoulder blades down and rotates them so you can raise your arms overhead safely; Y-raises target it.

Best exercises for the lower trapezius

  1. Incline Y-RaiseDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  2. Scapular Pull-UpOther · beginner · isolation
  3. Dumbbell ScaptionDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  4. Cross-Body Cable Y-RaiseCable · intermediate
  5. Bent Over Barbell RowBarbell · beginner · compound
  6. Bent Over One-Arm Long Bar RowBarbell · beginner · compound
