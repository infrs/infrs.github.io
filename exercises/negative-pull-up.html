How to do it

  1. Stand on a box or chair under a pull-up bar and grab it with an overhand grip.
  2. Jump or step up so your chin is above the bar.
  3. Lower yourself as slowly as you can, aiming for 3–5 seconds, until your arms are straight.
  4. Step back onto the box and repeat.
