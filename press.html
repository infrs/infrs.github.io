About the app

Body Atlas turns a 3D model of the human body into a guide to training and nutrition. Tap a muscle to get exercises for it, with start and end pictures and the helper muscles each move works. Tap an organ or a bone to see the nutrients it relies on and the foods richest in them. It also makes a weekly training plan and has a journal for workouts, cardio, food and water. Every feature is free, there's no account, and everything is stored on the phone.

About the developer

Body Atlas is made by one developer, infrs. I wanted a fitness app that shows you the body itself in 3D where you can find exactly the muscles you want to target instead of just a list, and that doesn't charge a subscription or any payments for any of it's features, so I built one. It's paid for by optional ads that people choose to watch, never forced ones.
