Best biceps exercises

Barbell Curl, Incline Dumbbell Curl and Hammer Curls are among the best exercises for the biceps. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the biceps.

What the biceps muscles do

The front of the upper arm: the two-headed biceps over the brachialis, with the small coracobrachialis near the armpit. They bend the elbow, and the biceps also turns the palm up, which is why curls with the palms up work it most.
