Foods high in vitamin B5 (pantothenic acid)

Helps your body make and break down fats and produce energy. The richest common sources per serving are chicken liver (cooked), beef liver (cooked) and shiitake mushrooms (cooked). The Daily Value is 5 mg a day for adults.

What vitamin B5 (pantothenic acid) does

Pantothenic acid is part of coenzyme A, which your body needs to make and burn fats, build cholesterol and hormones, and get energy from food.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 5 mg a day for adults.

Tips

  • Found in almost every food; liver, mushrooms, fish, eggs, avocado and whole grains are richest.
  • Processing, canning and freezing reduce it, so fresh, whole foods keep more.

Good to know

  • Its name comes from the Greek "pantothen", meaning "from everywhere".
  • Because it’s in so many foods, a real shortage is very rare.
