Hypothenar muscles: what it does and how to train it

The little-finger side of the palm. The best exercises for it include Farmer’s Carry, Palms-Up Barbell Wrist Curl Over A Bench and Palms-Down Wrist Curl Over A Bench.

What it does

The pad on the little-finger side of the palm. Its small muscles move the little finger and cup the palm, which helps you grip round objects.

Best exercises for the hypothenar muscles

  1. Farmer’s CarryDumbbell · beginner · compound
  2. Palms-Up Barbell Wrist Curl Over A BenchBarbell · beginner · isolation
  3. Palms-Down Wrist Curl Over A BenchBarbell · beginner · isolation
  4. Reverse Barbell CurlBarbell · beginner · isolation
  5. Hammer CurlsDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  6. Dead HangOther · beginner · isolation
