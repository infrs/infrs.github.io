Subscapularis: what it does and how to train it

Rotator cuff, on the front of the shoulder blade against the ribs. The best exercises for it include Barbell Shoulder Press, Dumbbell Shoulder Press and Side Lateral Raise.

What it does

The largest rotator cuff muscle, lying on the front of the shoulder blade between it and the ribs. It turns the arm inward and holds the ball of the upper arm in its socket, especially when the arm is raised.

Best exercises for the subscapularis

  1. Barbell Shoulder PressBarbell · intermediate · compound
  2. Dumbbell Shoulder PressDumbbell · intermediate · compound
  3. Side Lateral RaiseDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  4. Face PullCable · intermediate · compound
  5. Arnold Dumbbell PressDumbbell · intermediate · compound
  6. Reverse FlyesDumbbell · beginner · isolation
