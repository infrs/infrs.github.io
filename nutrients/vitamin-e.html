Foods high in vitamin E (tocopherol)

An antioxidant that protects your cells from damage. The richest common sources per serving are wheat germ oil, almond milk (unsweetened, fortified) and sunflower seeds. The Daily Value is 15 mg a day for adults.

What vitamin E (tocopherol) does

Vitamin E is a fat-soluble antioxidant that protects cell membranes from damage by free radicals. It also supports your immune system and helps blood vessels widen.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 15 mg a day for adults. The safe upper limit for adults is 1000 mg a day from supplements and fortified foods.

Tips

  • Nuts, seeds and plant oils are the richest sources. A handful of sunflower seeds or almonds covers a large part of your day.
  • Eat it with meals that contain some fat so you absorb it.
  • Store oils, nuts and seeds away from heat and light, which break vitamin E down.

What blocks it

  • Meals with no fat at all: Vitamin E dissolves in fat, and without any in the meal very little of it is absorbed.

Good to know

  • A real shortage is rare and usually comes from conditions that block fat absorption.
  • High-dose supplements can raise the risk of bleeding, especially with blood thinners.
