How to do it

  1. Sit with your legs straight and your hands on the floor (or on parallettes) beside your hips.
  2. Push down to lift your hips off the floor, as in an L-sit.
  3. Lean your shoulders back and raise your straight legs higher than your hips, toward your face, making a V.
  4. Hold with your arms locked, then lower to an L and sit down.

It needs flexible hamstrings and strong hip flexors as well as strength: pike compression lifts help with both.
