Flat Machine Press

How to do it

  1. Start position: Elbows bent at roughly 90°, hands just outside your chest.
  2. Keep wrists straight and aligned with your forearms.
  3. Pressing phase (concentric): Push the handles forward until your arms are almost fully extended — don’t lock out.
  4. Focus on squeezing your chest at the end of the movement.
  5. Returning phase (eccentric): Slowly bring the handles back toward your chest, feeling the stretch in your pecs.
  6. Maintain tension — don’t let the weight stack touch down between reps.
