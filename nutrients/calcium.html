Foods high in calcium

Builds bones and teeth and helps muscles, nerves and the heart work. The richest common sources per serving are tofu (firm, calcium-set), almond milk (unsweetened, fortified) and protein shake (whey with milk). The Daily Value is 1300 mg a day for adults.

What calcium does

About 99% of your body’s calcium is in your bones and teeth. The rest helps muscles contract, nerves send signals, blood clot and your heart beat. If you don’t eat enough, your body takes calcium from your bones.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 1300 mg a day for adults. The safe upper limit for adults is 2500 mg a day.

Tips

  • Vitamin D helps you absorb it.
  • You absorb calcium best in amounts of 500 mg or less at a time, so spread it over the day.
  • Spinach contains calcium, but oxalates block most of it. Kale, bok choy, broccoli and dairy are absorbed much better.
  • Calcium blocks some iron absorption, so take calcium supplements away from iron-rich meals.

What blocks it

  • Wheat bran: Pure wheat bran is the only food known to lower the calcium you absorb from other foods eaten with it. Bran in bread matters much less.
  • Spinach, rhubarb and beet greens: Their oxalates lock up calcium, mostly their own, so don’t count on them for calcium.
  • Lots of coffee: Caffeine slightly lowers how much calcium you absorb. A cup or two a day makes little difference.

Good to know

  • Bone mass peaks around age 30. Calcium and exercise when you’re young help protect against osteoporosis later.
  • Calcium-set tofu and canned sardines with their bones are rich non-dairy sources.
