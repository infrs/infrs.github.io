Band Terminal Knee Extension

Equipment
Bands
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Main muscles

  • Quads

How to do it

  1. This is an end range strengthening exercise for the knee extensors.
  2. Start by looping a band around the back of one knee and anchoring the other end to a stable surface in front of you.
  3. Stand in a split stance with the banded knee bent and placed as the front leg.
  4. Straighten the knee to pull against the bands resistance and maintain a contraction in your quadriceps for up to 5 seconds before releasing.
