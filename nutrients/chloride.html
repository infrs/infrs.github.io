What chloride does

Chloride is the other half of salt (sodium chloride). It works with sodium and potassium to keep the right amount of fluid in and around your cells and to carry nerve signals, and your stomach uses it to make the acid that digests food and kills germs.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 2300 mg a day for adults.

Tips

  • Nearly all of it comes from salt, so getting enough sodium means getting enough chloride.
  • Tomatoes, celery, lettuce, olives, rye and seaweed have some of their own.

Good to know

  • A shortage is very rare. It can follow a lot of vomiting, diarrhea or heavy sweating.
  • The adult upper limit is 3,600 mg a day. Too much comes with too much salt, so keeping sodium down keeps chloride down too.
