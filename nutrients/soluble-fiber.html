Foods high in soluble fiber

Fiber that dissolves into a gel, slowing digestion and lowering cholesterol. The richest common sources per serving are barley (cooked), oat bran (cooked) and guava.

What soluble fiber does

Soluble fiber soaks up water in your gut and turns into a gel. It slows how fast sugar reaches your blood, keeps you full longer, and carries cholesterol-rich bile out, which lowers LDL cholesterol. Your gut bacteria ferment it into compounds that feed the cells of your colon.

How much you need

No set amount. About 5–10 g a day helps lower LDL cholesterol

Tips

  • Oats, barley, beans, lentils, apples, citrus and flaxseed are rich in it.

Good to know

  • The beta-glucan in oats and barley is the soluble fiber behind their heart-health claims: about 3 g a day lowers cholesterol.
