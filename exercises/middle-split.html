How to do it

  1. Stand with your feet wide apart, toes pointing forward, and put your hands on the floor or on blocks in front of you.
  2. Slowly slide your feet further apart, keeping your knees straight.
  3. As you get lower, move your hips back so they stay in line with your feet, and support yourself on your hands or forearms.
  4. Hold, then use your hands to bring your feet back together slowly.

Go down very slowly and stop at a strong stretch, not pain: the inner thigh muscles strain easily.
