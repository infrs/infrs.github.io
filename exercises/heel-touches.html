Heel Touches

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Especially targets

  • External obliqueSides of the waist
  • Internal obliqueUnder the external oblique; twists and bends the trunk

Main muscles

  • Abs

How to do it

  1. Starting Position: Lie on your back on an exercise mat.
  2. Bend your knees, keeping your feet flat on the floor and hip-width apart.
  3. Place your arms along your sides, palms facing inward, just off the floor.
  4. Engage Core: Lift your head, shoulders, and upper back slightly off the ground.
  5. Keep your neck neutral and chin slightly tucked.
  6. Perform the Movement: Side-bend to the right, reaching your right hand toward your right heel.
  7. Return to the center, then side-bend to the left, reaching your left hand toward your left heel.
  8. Continue alternating sides in a controlled manner.
  9. Breathing: Exhale as you reach toward your heel and inhale as you return to the center.
