Best inner thighs exercises

Copenhagen Plank, Short-Lever Copenhagen Plank and Cossack Squat are among the best exercises for the inner thighs. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the inner thighs.

What the inner thighs muscles do

The muscles on the inside of the thigh pull the legs together and steady the hips and knees when you squat, run or change direction.
