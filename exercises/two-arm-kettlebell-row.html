Two-Arm Kettlebell Row

Equipment
Kettlebells
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength
Movement
Compound (several joints)
Force
Pull

Especially targets

  • Middle trapeziusTransverse part, across the shoulder blades
  • Rhomboid majorBetween the shoulder blades, under the trapezius
  • Rhomboid minorAbove the rhomboid major, under the trapezius

Main muscles

  • Middle back

Also works

  • Biceps
  • Lats

How to do it

  1. Place two kettlebells in front of your feet. Bend your knees slightly and then push your butt out as much as possible as you bend over to get in the starting position.
  2. Grab both kettlebells and pull them to your stomach, retracting your shoulder blades and flexing the elbows. Keep your back straight. Lower and repeat.
