Thenar muscles: what it does and how to train it

The ball of the thumb. The best exercises for it include Plate Pinch, Farmer’s Carry and Standing Olympic Plate Hand Squeeze.

What it does

The fleshy ball at the base of the thumb, made of a few small muscles. They bring the thumb across the palm to touch each fingertip, which makes pinching and fine grips possible.

Best exercises for the thenar muscles

  1. Plate PinchOther · intermediate · isolation
  2. Farmer’s CarryDumbbell · beginner · compound
  3. Standing Olympic Plate Hand SqueezeOther · beginner · isolation
  4. Cable Wrist CurlCable · beginner · isolation
