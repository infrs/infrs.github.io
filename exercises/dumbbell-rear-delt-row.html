Dumbbell Rear Delt Row

Equipment
Dumbbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Especially targets

  • Posterior deltoidBack of the shoulder
  • Middle trapeziusTransverse part, across the shoulder blades
  • Rhomboid majorBetween the shoulder blades, under the trapezius
  • Rhomboid minorAbove the rhomboid major, under the trapezius

Main muscles

  • Shoulders
  • Biceps
  • Traps

Also works

  • Middle back
  • Forearms & hands

How to do it

  1. Hold a dumbbell in each hand. Bend at your hips to make your back almost parallel to the floor. Let your arms hang down.
  2. Keep your arms in line with your shoulders. bend and lift your elbows out to the sides until your upper arms are parallel to the floor.
  3. Slowly return and repeat
