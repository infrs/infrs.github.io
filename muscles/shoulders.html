Best shoulders exercises

Barbell Shoulder Press, Dumbbell Shoulder Press and Side Lateral Raise are among the best exercises for the shoulders. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the shoulders.

What the shoulders muscles do

The deltoid caps the shoulder in three parts, front, side and back, and lifts the arm in every direction. Under it, the rotator cuff holds the ball of the upper arm in its shallow socket, so strong, balanced shoulders are less prone to injury.

  • InfraspinatusRotator cuff, on the back of the shoulder blade
