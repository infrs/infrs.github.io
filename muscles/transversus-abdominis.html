Transversus abdominis: what it does and how to train it

The deepest layer of the belly; braces the trunk like a belt. The best exercises for it include Hanging Leg Raise, Cable Crunch and Ab Roller.

What it does

The deepest layer of the belly, with fibers running around the waist like a belt. It doesn’t move the trunk; it tightens before you lift or push, steadying the spine. Planks and drawing the belly in train it.

Best exercises for the transversus abdominis

  1. Hanging Leg RaiseOther · expert · isolation
  2. Cable CrunchCable · beginner · isolation
  3. Ab RollerOther · intermediate · compound
  4. PlankNo equipment · beginner · isolation
  5. Pallof PressCable · beginner · isolation
  6. Dead BugNo equipment · beginner · compound
