External oblique: what it does and how to train it

Sides of the waist. The best exercises for it include Pallof Press, Side Plank and Standing Cable Wood Chop.

What it does

The outermost muscle on the sides of the waist, its fibers running down and forward like hands in your pockets. It twists and side-bends the trunk, and stops it twisting under load, as in side planks and Pallof presses.

Best exercises for the external oblique

  1. Pallof PressCable · beginner · isolation
  2. Side PlankNo equipment · beginner · isolation
  3. Standing Cable Wood ChopCable · beginner · compound
  4. Russian TwistNo equipment · intermediate · compound
  5. Alternate Heel TouchersNo equipment · beginner · isolation
  6. Barbell Side BendBarbell · beginner · isolation
