Deep hip rotators: what it does and how to train it

Piriformis and its neighbours, under the gluteus maximus; turn the thigh out. The best exercises for it include Barbell Hip Thrust, Romanian Deadlift and Bulgarian Split Squat.

What it does

Six small muscles under the gluteus maximus: the piriformis, the two obturators, the two gemelli and the quadratus femoris. They turn the thigh out and hold the ball of the hip in its socket. A tight piriformis can press on the sciatic nerve.

Best exercises for the deep hip rotators

  1. Barbell Hip ThrustBarbell · intermediate · compound
  2. Romanian DeadliftBarbell · intermediate · compound
  3. Bulgarian Split SquatNo equipment · intermediate · compound
  4. Barbell SquatBarbell · beginner · compound
  5. Butt Lift (Bridge)No equipment · beginner · isolation
  6. One-Legged Cable KickbackCable · intermediate · isolation
