Face Pull

Equipment
Cable
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength
Movement
Compound (several joints)
Force
Pull

Especially targets

  • Posterior deltoidBack of the shoulder
  • Middle trapeziusTransverse part, across the shoulder blades
  • Rhomboid majorBetween the shoulder blades, under the trapezius
  • Rhomboid minorAbove the rhomboid major, under the trapezius

Main muscles

  • Shoulders

Also works

  • Middle back

How to do it

  1. Facing a high pulley with a rope or dual handles attached, pull the weight directly towards your face, separating your hands as you do so. Keep your upper arms parallel to the ground.
