Best middle back exercises

Bent Over Barbell Row, Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row and Seated Cable Rows are among the best exercises for the middle back. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the middle back.

What the middle back muscles do

Between and below the shoulder blades: the middle and lower trapezius and the two rhomboids. They pull the shoulder blades together and down, holding your shoulders back and steady for rows, pull-ups and good posture.
