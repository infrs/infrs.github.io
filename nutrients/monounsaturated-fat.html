Foods high in monounsaturated fat

A heart-friendly fat found in olive oil, avocados and most nuts. The richest common sources per serving are macadamia nuts, hazelnuts and sunflower seed butter.

What monounsaturated fat does

Monounsaturated fats are liquid at room temperature. When they replace saturated fat, they help lower LDL cholesterol. They’re the main fat in olive oil, avocados, almonds, hazelnuts and macadamias.

Tips

  • Use olive or canola oil instead of butter for cooking and dressings.

Good to know

  • Olive oil, the base of the Mediterranean diet, is about three-quarters monounsaturated fat.
