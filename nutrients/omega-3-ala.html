What omega-3 ALA (alpha-linolenic acid) does

Alpha-linolenic acid is the omega-3 fat found in plants. It’s essential: your body can’t make it. It uses it for energy, for cell membranes, and to make small amounts of the longer omega-3s, EPA and DHA.

How much you need

About 1.1–1.6 g

Tips

  • Grind flaxseeds before eating them: whole seeds often pass through undigested.
  • Stir a spoonful of ground flax or chia into oatmeal, yogurt or smoothies, or snack on walnuts.

Good to know

  • Adults need about 1.1 g (women) to 1.6 g (men) a day.
  • Only a small share of ALA is turned into EPA, and even less into DHA, so it doesn’t fully replace fish omega-3s.
