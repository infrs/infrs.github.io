Diamond Push Ups

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Main muscles

  • Chest

Also works

  • Triceps

How to do it

  1. Start: Get into a plank. Place hands close together under your chest so thumbs and index fingers form a diamond shape.
  2. Lower: Bend your elbows to lower your chest toward your hands. Keep your body straight. Push: Push back up until arms are fully extended.
  3. New note Keep elbows close to your body and core tight.
