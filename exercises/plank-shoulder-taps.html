Plank Shoulder Taps

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Especially targets

  • Transversus abdominisThe deepest layer of the belly; braces the trunk like a belt

Main muscles

  • Shoulders
  • Glutes
  • Abs

Also works

  • Lats

How to do it

  1. In the correct plank position, place your feet slightly wider than shoulder-width apart.
  2. Alternately lift and touch the opposite shoulder with one hand.
