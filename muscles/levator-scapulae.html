Levator scapulae: what it does and how to train it

Side and back of the neck; lifts the shoulder blade. The best exercises for it include Dumbbell Shrug, Barbell Shrug and Barbell Shrug Behind The Back.

What it does

Runs from the side of the neck down to the top corner of the shoulder blade. It lifts the shoulder blade, and a stiff neck after sleeping awkwardly often comes from it.

Best exercises for the levator scapulae

  1. Dumbbell ShrugDumbbell · beginner · isolation

  3. Barbell Shrug Behind The BackBarbell · beginner · isolation
