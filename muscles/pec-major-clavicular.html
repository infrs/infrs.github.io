Upper pectoralis major: what it does and how to train it

Clavicular head, just under the collarbone. The best exercises for it include Barbell Incline Bench Press - Medium Grip, Incline Dumbbell Press and Low-to-High Cable Fly.

What it does

The upper part of the chest, starting from the inner half of the collarbone. It lifts the arm forward and up as well as across, so incline presses and low-to-high flyes work it most.

Best exercises for the upper pectoralis major

  1. Barbell Incline Bench Press - Medium GripBarbell · beginner · compound
  2. Incline Dumbbell PressDumbbell · beginner · compound
  3. Low-to-High Cable FlyCable · beginner · isolation
  4. Incline Dumbbell FlyesDumbbell · beginner · compound
  5. Decline Push-UpOther · beginner · compound
