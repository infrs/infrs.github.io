Upper trapezius: what it does and how to train it

Descending part, from the neck to the shoulder. The best exercises for it include Barbell Shrug, Dumbbell Shrug and Farmer’s Carry.

What it does

The upper part of the trapezius, from the base of the skull and neck out to the collarbone. It lifts and rotates the shoulder blades, as in shrugs and carrying, and tenses up under stress.

Best exercises for the upper trapezius

  1. Barbell ShrugBarbell · beginner · isolation
  2. Dumbbell ShrugDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  3. Farmer’s CarryDumbbell · beginner · compound
  4. Barbell Shrug Behind The BackBarbell · beginner · isolation
  5. Cable ShrugsCable · beginner · isolation
  6. Calf-Machine Shoulder ShrugMachine · beginner · isolation
