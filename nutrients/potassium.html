Foods high in potassium

Balances fluids and supports healthy blood pressure and heart rhythm. The richest common sources per serving are fufu, potato (baked, with skin) and carrot juice. The Daily Value is 4700 mg a day for adults.

What potassium does

Potassium works with sodium to balance the fluids in your body and keep nerves and muscles, including your heart, working. Getting more potassium helps lower blood pressure, especially if you eat a lot of salt.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 4700 mg a day for adults.

Tips

  • Potatoes, beans, lentils, leafy greens, bananas and dried apricots are good sources.
  • Boiling vegetables leaches potassium into the water; steaming, baking or using the cooking water keeps it.
  • People with kidney disease, or on some blood pressure medicines, may need to limit potassium. Follow your doctor’s advice.

Good to know

  • A baked potato with its skin has about twice the potassium of a banana.
  • Most adults get far less than the 4,700 mg Daily Value.
