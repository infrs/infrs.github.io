Thread the Needle

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Beginner
Type
Stretch

Main muscles

  • Middle back

Also works

  • Shoulders

How to do it

  1. Start on your hands and knees, hands under your shoulders and knees under your hips.
  2. Slide one arm under your body along the floor, palm up, lowering that shoulder and the side of your head to the floor.
  3. Let your upper back twist and hold, breathing slowly.
  4. Unwind, reach the same arm up toward the ceiling, then switch sides.
