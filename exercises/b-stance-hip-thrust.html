B-Stance Hip Thrust

Equipment
Barbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength
Movement
Isolation (one joint)
Force
Push

Main muscles

  • Glutes

Also works

  • Hamstrings

How to do it

  1. Set up like a barbell hip thrust: upper back on a bench, padded bar across your hips.
  2. Put one foot flat under the knee and the other a little forward, resting only on its heel.
  3. Drive mostly through the planted foot to lift your hips until your body is straight.
  4. Lower under control. Finish all reps, then switch sides.

A step between two-leg and single-leg hip thrusts.
