Best triceps exercises

Close-Grip Barbell Bench Press, Dips - Triceps Version and Lying Triceps Press are among the best exercises for the triceps. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the triceps.

What the triceps muscles do

The back of the upper arm, about two-thirds of its muscle. The three heads of the triceps straighten the elbow in every push, from push-ups to overhead presses, helped by the small anconeus.

Muscles of the triceps

  • Triceps brachii (long head)Inner back of the arm; the biggest head
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)Outer back of the arm, the "horseshoe"
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)Deep head, just above the elbow
  • AnconeusSmall muscle at the back of the elbow
