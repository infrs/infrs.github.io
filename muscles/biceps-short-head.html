Biceps brachii (short head): what it does and how to train it

Inner head of the biceps. The best exercises for it include Preacher Curl, Concentration Curls and Spider Curl.

What it does

The inner head of the biceps, starting from a small hook of the shoulder blade at the front of the shoulder. Curls with the arms in front of the body, like preacher and concentration curls, tend to favor it.

Best exercises for the biceps brachii (short head)

  1. Preacher CurlBarbell · beginner · isolation
  2. Concentration CurlsDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  3. Spider CurlBarbell · beginner · isolation
  4. Cable Preacher CurlCable · beginner · isolation
  5. Incline Inner Biceps CurlDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  6. Machine Preacher CurlsMachine · beginner · isolation
