  • Traps

How to do it

  1. Lie face down on a bench set to about 30–45°, holding light dumbbells with your thumbs up.
  2. Let your arms hang straight down.
  3. Raise your arms up and out into a Y shape until they are in line with your body.
  4. Lower slowly.

Use light weights. It trains the lower trapezius, which helps you keep your shoulder blades down.
