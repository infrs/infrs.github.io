Foods high in choline

Supports the brain, liver and muscles. The richest common sources per serving are beef liver (cooked), chicken liver (cooked) and egg (hard-boiled). The Daily Value is 550 mg a day for adults.

What choline does

Choline is used to build cell membranes and to make acetylcholine, a messenger for memory, mood and muscle control. It also helps your liver move fat out, which keeps fat from building up there.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 550 mg a day for adults. The safe upper limit for adults is 3500 mg a day.

Tips

  • Eggs, liver, fish, chicken and soybeans are top sources. One egg gives about a quarter of the Daily Value.
  • Needs rise during pregnancy and breastfeeding, and many prenatal vitamins contain little or none.

Good to know

  • Your body makes some choline, but not enough, so you need it from food too.
  • It was only recognized as an essential nutrient in 1998.
