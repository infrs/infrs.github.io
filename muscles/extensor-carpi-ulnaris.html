Extensor carpi ulnaris: what it does and how to train it

Straightens the wrist, little-finger side. The best exercises for it include Palms-Down Wrist Curl Over A Bench, Wrist Roller and Reverse Barbell Curl.

What it does

Runs along the little-finger side of the back of the forearm. It straightens the wrist, tilts it toward the little finger, and steadies the wrist when you make a fist.
