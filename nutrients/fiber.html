Foods high in fiber

Feeds your gut bacteria and supports digestion, fullness, cholesterol and blood sugar. The richest common sources per serving are navy beans (cooked), guava and split peas (cooked). The Daily Value is 28 g a day for adults.

What fiber does

Fiber is the part of plant foods you can’t digest. Soluble fiber (oats, beans, apples) forms a gel that slows digestion and helps lower LDL cholesterol. Insoluble fiber (whole grains, vegetable skins) adds bulk and keeps you regular. Your gut bacteria ferment fiber into compounds that nourish the lining of your gut.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 28 g a day for adults.

Tips

  • Increase it gradually over a few weeks and drink plenty of water, to avoid bloating.
  • Eat fruit and potatoes with the skin, and swap white bread, rice and pasta for whole-grain versions.
  • Beans and lentils are among the richest sources: half a cup cooked gives about 6–10 g.

Good to know

  • In the US, over 90% of women and 97% of men don’t get the recommended amount.
  • Diets rich in fiber are linked to a lower risk of heart disease, type 2 diabetes and colon cancer.
