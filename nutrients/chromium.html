Foods high in chromium

Helps insulin move sugar from your blood into your cells. The richest common sources per serving are broccoli (raw), english muffin (fortified) and potato (baked, with skin). The Daily Value is 35 µg a day for adults.

What chromium does

Chromium helps insulin do its work, moving sugar from your blood into your cells. It’s needed in tiny amounts, and how much a food has varies a lot with where it was grown and how it was made.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 35 µg a day for adults.

Tips

  • Broccoli, whole grains, meat and grape juice are among the better sources.

Good to know

  • A shortage is very rare in people who eat a varied diet.
  • Few foods have been measured for chromium, so these amounts are rough estimates for the ones that have.
