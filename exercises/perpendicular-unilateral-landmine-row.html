Perpendicular Unilateral Landmine Row

Equipment
Barbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Especially targets

  • Lateral deltoidSide of the shoulder
  • Middle trapeziusTransverse part, across the shoulder blades
  • Rhomboid majorBetween the shoulder blades, under the trapezius
  • Rhomboid minorAbove the rhomboid major, under the trapezius

Main muscles

  • Shoulders
  • Traps

Also works

  • Middle back
  • Biceps

How to do it

  1. Using a landmine attachment with the barbell running at 90 degrees to either side, hinge slightly at the hips and grip the barbell by the plate sleeve.
  2. Raise the barbell to chest height, with your upper arm parallel to the floor at the top of the movement.
