Seal Row

Equipment
Barbell
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength
Movement
Compound (several joints)
Force
Pull

Especially targets

  • Middle trapeziusTransverse part, across the shoulder blades
  • Rhomboid majorBetween the shoulder blades, under the trapezius
  • Rhomboid minorAbove the rhomboid major, under the trapezius

Main muscles

  • Middle back

Also works

  • Lats
  • Biceps
  • Shoulders

How to do it

  1. Lie face down on a high flat bench with a barbell (or two dumbbells) on the floor under you.
  2. Grab the bar a little wider than shoulder width and lift it off the floor with straight arms.
  3. Row it up until it touches the underside of the bench.
  4. Lower until your arms are straight.
