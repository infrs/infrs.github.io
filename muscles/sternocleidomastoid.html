Sternocleidomastoid: what it does and how to train it

Front and side of the neck; turns and bends the head. The best exercises for it include Lying Face Up Plate Neck Resistance, Isometric Neck Exercise - Front And Back and Isometric Neck Exercise - Sides.

What it does

The strap you can see on each side of the neck when you turn your head, running from behind the ear to the breastbone and collarbone. One side turns the head the other way; both together bend it forward.
