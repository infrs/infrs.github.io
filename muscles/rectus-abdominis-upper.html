Upper rectus abdominis: what it does and how to train it

The six-pack above the navel. The best exercises for it include Cable Crunch, Crunches and Ab Roller.

What it does

The part of the six-pack above the navel. It curls the ribcage toward the pelvis, as in crunches and sit-ups. The rectus abdominis is one long muscle, so every ab exercise trains all of it, but some favor one end.

Best exercises for the upper rectus abdominis

  1. Cable CrunchCable · beginner · isolation
  2. CrunchesNo equipment · beginner · isolation
  3. Ab RollerOther · intermediate · compound
  4. 3/4 Sit-UpNo equipment · beginner · compound
  5. Cable Seated CrunchCable · beginner · isolation
  6. Crunch - Hands OverheadNo equipment · beginner · isolation
