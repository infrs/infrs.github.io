How to do it

  1. Lie on a bench and grab its edge (or something solid) behind your head.
  2. Lift your body so only your upper back touches the bench, body straight.
  3. Lower slowly as one straight line until you hover just above the bench.
  4. Pull back up to the top without bending at the hips.
