Tibialis posterior: what it does and how to train it

Deep in the back of the calf; holds up the arch of the foot. The best exercises for it include Standing Calf Raises, Seated Calf Raise and Single-Leg Calf Raise.

What it does

The deepest muscle in the back of the calf. Its tendon passes behind the inner ankle to the sole, where it holds up the arch of the foot and turns the sole inward; when it tires, the arch can flatten.
