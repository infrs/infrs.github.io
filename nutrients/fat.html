Foods high in fat

Supplies energy and essential fats, and helps you absorb vitamins A, D, E and K. The richest common sources per serving are umm ali (pastry and milk pudding), tiramisu and cheesecake. The Daily Value is 78 g a day for adults.

What fat does

Fat is the most concentrated source of energy, and it builds every cell membrane and many hormones. You need it to absorb the fat-soluble vitamins A, D, E and K, and plant pigments such as beta-carotene. The type of fat matters more for your heart than the total amount.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 78 g a day for adults.

Tips

  • Favor unsaturated fats from olive oil, nuts, seeds, avocado and fish over saturated fats from butter, fatty meat and coconut oil.
  • Add a little fat, like olive oil or nuts, to salads and vegetables: it helps you absorb their vitamins.

Good to know

  • Each gram of fat has 9 calories, more than twice as much as protein or carbohydrates.
  • Very low-fat diets can make it hard to get enough vitamin E and essential fats.
