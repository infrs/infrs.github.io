Barbell Bench Press - Medium Grip, Incline Dumbbell Press and Dips - Chest Version are among the best exercises for the chest. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the chest.

What the chest muscles do

The pectoralis major fans across the front of the chest, from the collarbone and breastbone to the upper arm, with the serratus anterior along the ribs below the armpit. Together they push your arms forward and across your body, as in a push-up, a bench press or a hug.
