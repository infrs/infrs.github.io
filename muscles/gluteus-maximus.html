Gluteus maximus: what it does and how to train it

The main buttock muscle. The best exercises for it include Barbell Hip Thrust, Romanian Deadlift and Bulgarian Split Squat.

What it does

The largest muscle in the body, shaping the buttocks. It extends the hip, powering you up from a squat, up stairs and forward when you sprint or jump, and helps keep the pelvis upright. Hip thrusts, squats and deadlifts train it.

Best exercises for the gluteus maximus

  1. Barbell Hip ThrustBarbell · intermediate · compound
  2. Romanian DeadliftBarbell · intermediate · compound
  3. Bulgarian Split SquatNo equipment · intermediate · compound
  4. Butt Lift (Bridge)No equipment · beginner · isolation
  5. Flutter KicksNo equipment · beginner · compound
  6. Frog PumpNo equipment · beginner · isolation
