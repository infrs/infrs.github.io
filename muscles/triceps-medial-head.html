Triceps brachii (medial head): what it does and how to train it

Deep head, just above the elbow. The best exercises for it include Reverse Grip Triceps Pushdown, Close-Grip Barbell Bench Press and One Arm Supinated Dumbbell Triceps Extension.

What it does

The deep head, mostly hidden under the other two, just above the elbow. It works in every elbow extension, light or heavy; reverse-grip pushdowns favor it a little.

Best exercises for the triceps brachii (medial head)

  1. Reverse Grip Triceps PushdownCable · beginner · isolation
  2. Close-Grip Barbell Bench PressBarbell · beginner · compound
  3. One Arm Supinated Dumbbell Triceps ExtensionDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  4. Reverse Triceps Bench PressBarbell · intermediate · compound
  5. Band Skull CrusherBands · beginner · isolation
  6. Bench DipsNo equipment · beginner · compound
