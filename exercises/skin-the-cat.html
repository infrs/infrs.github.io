How to do it

  1. Hang from rings or a bar with your hands shoulder width apart.
  2. Tuck your knees and lift them up and through your arms, rolling backward until your feet point down behind you.
  3. Lower your hips toward the floor as far as is comfortable, feeling the stretch in the front of your shoulders.
  4. Reverse the movement slowly, pulling your knees back through your arms to the hang.

Go only as deep as your shoulders allow, and build depth over weeks. Rings are kinder to the shoulders than a bar.
