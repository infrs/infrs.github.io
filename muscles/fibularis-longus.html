Fibularis longus: what it does and how to train it

Outer lower leg (also called peroneus longus). The best exercises for it include Standing Calf Raises, Seated Calf Raise and Single-Leg Calf Raise.

What it does

Runs down the outer side of the lower leg, and its tendon wraps under the foot. It turns the sole outward and helps hold the arch, steadying the ankle against rolling over.
