Longissimus: what it does and how to train it

Middle column of the erector spinae. The best exercises for it include Barbell Deadlift, Hyperextensions (Back Extensions) and Good Morning.

What it does

The middle and longest column of the erector spinae, reaching from the pelvis up to the skull. It does much of the work of holding the spine straight when you bend and lift.

Best exercises for the longissimus

  1. Barbell DeadliftBarbell · intermediate · compound
  2. Hyperextensions (Back Extensions)Other · beginner · isolation
  3. Good MorningBarbell · intermediate · compound
  4. Bird DogNo equipment · beginner · compound
  5. Arch Body HoldNo equipment · beginner · compound
  6. Stiff Leg Barbell Good MorningBarbell · beginner · compound
