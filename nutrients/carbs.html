Foods high in carbohydrates

Your body’s main fuel, especially for the brain and hard exercise. The richest common sources per serving are soft pretzel, fufu and chocolate chip muffin. The Daily Value is 275 g a day for adults.

What carbohydrates does

Carbohydrates are broken down into glucose, the fuel your brain and muscles use most readily. What you don’t use right away is stored as glycogen in your liver and muscles, ready for exercise or for the hours between meals.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 275 g a day for adults.

Tips

  • Choose whole grains, legumes, fruit and vegetables: they bring fiber, vitamins and minerals along with the energy.
  • Eating carbs with protein, fat or fiber slows how fast they raise your blood sugar.
  • Before long or hard exercise, a carbohydrate-rich meal helps you perform better.

Good to know

  • The recommended minimum of 130 g a day is based on how much glucose your brain uses.
  • Each gram of carbohydrate has 4 calories.
