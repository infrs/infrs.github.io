Serratus anterior: what it does and how to train it

Along the ribs, below the armpit. The best exercises for it include Scapular Push-Up, Pushups and Alternating Floor Press.

What it does

Saw-toothed strips along the side of the ribs, under the armpit. It pulls the shoulder blade forward around the ribs and holds it flat against them; when it’s weak, the shoulder blade sticks out. An extra push at the top of a push-up trains it.

Best exercises for the serratus anterior

  1. Scapular Push-UpNo equipment · beginner · isolation
  2. PushupsNo equipment · beginner · compound
  3. Alternating Floor PressKettlebells · beginner · compound
  4. Barbell Bench Press - Medium GripBarbell · beginner · compound
  5. Barbell Incline Bench Press - Medium GripBarbell · beginner · compound
  6. Bench Press - With BandsBands · beginner · compound
