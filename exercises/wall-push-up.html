Wall Push-Up

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Beginner
Type
Strength
Movement
Compound (several joints)
Force
Push

Especially targets

  • Middle pectoralis majorSternocostal head, the bulk of the chest

Main muscles

  • Chest

Also works

  • Shoulders
  • Triceps

How to do it

  1. Stand an arm’s length from a wall and put your hands on it at shoulder height, a little wider than your shoulders.
  2. Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels and your core tight.
  3. Bend your elbows to bring your chest toward the wall, elbows angled about 45° from your body.
  4. Push the wall away until your arms are straight again.
