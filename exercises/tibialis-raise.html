Tibialis Raise

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Beginner
Type
Strength
Movement
Isolation (one joint)
Force
Pull

Especially targets

  • Tibialis anteriorFront of the shin; lifts the foot

Main muscles

  • Calves & shins

How to do it

  1. Lean your back against a wall with your heels about a foot in front of it.
  2. Keeping your legs straight, lift your toes as high as you can toward your shins.
  3. Pause at the top.
  4. Lower your toes slowly, stopping just before they touch the floor.

Strengthens the front of the shin (tibialis anterior), which can help with shin splints.
