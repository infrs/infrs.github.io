Foam Roller Thoracic Extension

Equipment
Foam roller
Level
Beginner
Type
Stretch

Main muscles

  • Middle back

Also works

  • Lats
  • Chest

How to do it

  1. Lie on your back with a foam roller across your upper back, just below your shoulder blades, knees bent and feet flat.
  2. Support your head with your hands and keep your hips on the floor.
  3. Let your upper back bend back over the roller, then curl back up.
  4. Move the roller a few centimeters further up and repeat. Keep it off your lower back.
