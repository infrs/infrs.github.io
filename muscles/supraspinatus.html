Supraspinatus: what it does and how to train it

Rotator cuff, above the spine of the shoulder blade. The best exercises for it include Dumbbell Scaption, Side Lateral Raise and Alternating Cable Shoulder Press.

What it does

Sits above the ridge of the shoulder blade and runs under the bony top of the shoulder to the upper arm. It starts lifting the arm out to the side and holds the ball in its socket. It’s the rotator cuff muscle most often injured.

Best exercises for the supraspinatus

  1. Dumbbell ScaptionDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  2. Side Lateral RaiseDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  3. Alternating Cable Shoulder PressCable · beginner · compound
  4. Alternating Deltoid RaiseDumbbell · beginner · isolation
