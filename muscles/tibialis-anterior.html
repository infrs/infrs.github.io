Tibialis anterior: what it does and how to train it

Front of the shin; lifts the foot. The best exercises for it include Tibialis Raise, Smith Machine Reverse Calf Raises and Anterior Tibialis-SMR.

What it does

The muscle you can feel along the front of the shin. It lifts the front of the foot so your toes clear the ground with each step and the foot lands softly.

Best exercises for the tibialis anterior

  1. Tibialis RaiseNo equipment · beginner · isolation
  2. Smith Machine Reverse Calf RaisesMachine · beginner · isolation
  3. Anterior Tibialis-SMROther · intermediate
  4. Posterior Tibialis StretchOther · intermediate
  5. Balance BoardOther · beginner · compound
  6. Barbell Seated Calf RaiseBarbell · beginner · isolation
