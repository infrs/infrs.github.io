What omega-3 EPA + DHA does

EPA and DHA are the long omega-3 fats found mainly in fish and seafood. DHA is a building block of your brain and retina, and both help keep your heart rhythm steady and your blood fats (triglycerides) low.

How much you need

About 250–500 mg

Tips

  • Eating fatty fish such as salmon, sardines, mackerel or herring twice a week covers most people’s needs.
  • Bake, grill or steam fish rather than deep-frying it.
  • Vegans can get EPA and DHA from algae oil. Fish get theirs from algae in the first place.

Good to know

  • There’s no official Daily Value, but many health groups suggest 250–500 mg of EPA and DHA a day.
  • Small fish such as sardines and anchovies have far less mercury than big ones such as swordfish and king mackerel.
