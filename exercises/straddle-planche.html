How to do it

  1. From an advanced tuck planche, straighten your legs out to the sides in a wide straddle.
  2. Keep your body level with the floor, arms locked and shoulders well in front of your hands.
  3. Squeeze your glutes and point your toes to keep your legs up.
  4. Hold, then bring your knees back in before lowering.

A wider straddle is easier, because it shortens the lever. Bring your legs closer together as you get stronger.
