Jefferson Curl

Main muscles

  • Hamstrings

How to do it

  1. Stand on a step or low box holding a light weight in front of your thighs, or no weight at all.
  2. Tuck your chin and slowly roll down one vertebra at a time, starting from your neck, letting the weight hang toward your feet.
  3. Keep your knees straight and go as far as is comfortable, feeling the stretch along your back and hamstrings.
  4. Roll back up just as slowly, stacking your lower back first and your head last.

Start with no weight or a very light one, and move slowly. Skip it if you have back pain or a back injury.
