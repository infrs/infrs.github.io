Key to the plates

  1. Body Anatomy

    Rotate and zoom a 3D anatomy model and tap the part you care about: muscles, organs or bones. Pick from the body, not a list.

    85 muscles · 17 muscle groups→

  2. Exercises

    Pick a muscle group, then the exact muscle, and get the best exercises for it, with start and end pictures and how to do each one.

    1251 exercises→

  3. Nutrients & Foods

    Pick a vitamin or mineral your body needs and see the foods richest in it per serving, and which organs and bones rely on it.

    40 nutrients · 880 foods→

  4. Plan & Journal

    A weekly training plan made from your goal and equipment, and a journal for workouts, cardio, food and water. It all stays on your phone.

    Free · no account→

Free, with optional support

The app is free to use. You can watch an optional video ad to unlock a colour theme, or buy a supporter badge or leave a tip if you want to help the app along.

Not medical advice

Body Atlas gives general fitness and nutrition information. It is not medical advice and is no substitute for a doctor, dietitian or qualified trainer. Talk to a professional before changing your exercise or diet, especially if you have a health condition.
