Best outer hips exercises

Side-Lying Hip Abduction, Thigh Abductor and Monster Walk are among the best exercises for the outer hips. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the outer hips.

What the outer hips muscles do

On the outer side of the hip: the gluteus medius and the tensor fasciae latae. They lift the leg out to the side and, more importantly, keep your pelvis level each time you stand on one leg, which happens with every step.
