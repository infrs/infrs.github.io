How to do it

  1. Pick up a heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in each hand, standing tall.
  2. Pull your shoulders back and down and brace your core.
  3. Walk with short, steady steps without letting the weights swing.
  4. Walk for a set distance or time, then set the weights down carefully.

Carry a weight in one hand only (suitcase carry) to work your obliques more.
