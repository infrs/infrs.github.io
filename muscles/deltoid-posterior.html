Posterior deltoid: what it does and how to train it

Back of the shoulder. The best exercises for it include Reverse Flyes, Face Pull and Cable Rear Delt Fly.

What it does

The back of the shoulder. It pulls the arm backward and turns it out, as in rear-delt flyes and face pulls. It’s often weaker than the front, which can round the shoulders forward.

Best exercises for the posterior deltoid

  1. Reverse FlyesDumbbell · beginner · isolation
  2. Face PullCable · intermediate · compound
  3. Cable Rear Delt FlyCable · beginner · isolation
  4. Seated Bent-Over Rear Delt RaiseDumbbell · intermediate · isolation
  5. Back Flyes - With BandsBands · beginner · compound
  6. Band Pull ApartBands · beginner · isolation
