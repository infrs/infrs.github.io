How to do it

  1. Kneel on a soft surface and anchor your heels under something solid, like a loaded bar, a couch or a partner’s hands.
  2. Keep a straight line from your knees to your head, hips pushed forward.
  3. Lower your body toward the floor as slowly as you can, resisting with your hamstrings.
  4. Catch yourself with your hands, push back up to kneeling and repeat.

One of the best-studied exercises for preventing hamstring strains.
