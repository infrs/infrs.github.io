Quadratus lumborum: what it does and how to train it

Deep in the lower back, from the lowest rib to the pelvis; bends the trunk to the side. The best exercises for it include Barbell Deadlift, Hyperextensions (Back Extensions) and Good Morning.

What it does

A deep, flat muscle on each side of the lower spine, between the lowest rib and the top of the pelvis. It bends the trunk to the side and hitches the hip up, and it’s often behind a stiff, aching lower back.

Best exercises for the quadratus lumborum

  1. Barbell DeadliftBarbell · intermediate · compound
  2. Hyperextensions (Back Extensions)Other · beginner · isolation
  3. Good MorningBarbell · intermediate · compound
  4. Romanian DeadliftBarbell · intermediate · compound
