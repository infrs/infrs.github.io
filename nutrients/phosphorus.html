Foods high in phosphorus

Builds bones and teeth and helps store and use energy. The richest common sources per serving are protein shake (whey with milk), hemp seeds and sardines (canned in oil). The Daily Value is 1250 mg a day for adults.

What phosphorus does

Phosphorus pairs with calcium to form bone mineral. It’s also part of ATP, the molecule your cells use to store and spend energy, and of DNA and every cell membrane.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 1250 mg a day for adults. The safe upper limit for adults is 4000 mg a day.

Tips

  • Most diets get plenty from protein-rich foods such as meat, fish, dairy, nuts and legumes.
  • In seeds, grains and beans much of it is locked in phytate, which you absorb less of. Soaking, sprouting and leavening release more.

Good to know

  • Phosphate additives in processed foods and colas are absorbed almost completely.
  • People with kidney disease may need to limit phosphorus. Follow your doctor’s advice.
