Foods high in manganese

Helps with bone formation, metabolism and antioxidant defenses. The richest common sources per serving are mussels (cooked), teff (cooked) and pine nuts. The Daily Value is 2.3 mg a day for adults.

What manganese does

Manganese is part of enzymes that build bone and cartilage, process carbohydrates, amino acids and cholesterol, and protect your cells from damage.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 2.3 mg a day for adults. The safe upper limit for adults is 11 mg a day.

Tips

  • Whole grains, nuts, legumes, tea and mussels are rich sources.

What blocks it

  • Iron supplements: Iron and manganese use the same way into your body, so a lot of iron means less manganese.

Good to know

  • A shortage from food is very rare.
  • For many people, tea is one of the main sources.
