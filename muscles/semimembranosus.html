Semimembranosus: what it does and how to train it

Inner hamstring, under the semitendinosus. The best exercises for it include Romanian Deadlift, Good Morning and Stiff-Legged Dumbbell Deadlift.

What it does

The deeper inner hamstring, broad and flat, under the semitendinosus. It extends the hip, bends the knee, and helps steady the back of the knee.

Best exercises for the semimembranosus

  1. Romanian DeadliftBarbell · intermediate · compound
  2. Good MorningBarbell · intermediate · compound
  3. Stiff-Legged Dumbbell DeadliftDumbbell · beginner · compound
  4. Band Good MorningBands · beginner · compound
  5. Band Good Morning (Pull Through)Bands · beginner · compound
  6. Single-Leg Romanian DeadliftNo equipment · beginner · compound
