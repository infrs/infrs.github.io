Foods high in every nutrient

Pick a nutrient to see the foods richest in it per serving, what it does in your body and how much you need each day.

Vitamins

  • Vitamin A (All forms)Needed for seeing in dim light, a strong immune system and healthy skin.
  • Vitamin B1 (Thiamin)Helps turn carbohydrates into energy and keeps nerves working.
  • Vitamin B2 (Riboflavin)Helps produce energy and keeps skin, eyes and cells healthy.
  • Vitamin B3 (Niacin)Supports energy use, skin, digestion and the nervous system.
  • Vitamin B5 (Pantothenic acid)Helps your body make and break down fats and produce energy.
  • Vitamin B7 (Biotin)Helps turn fat, carbs and protein into energy, and supports hair, skin and nails.
  • Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxine)Needed to use protein, make brain messengers and keep immunity strong.
  • Vitamin B9 (Folate)Needed to make DNA and red blood cells; especially important before and during pregnancy.
  • Vitamin B12 (Cobalamin)Keeps nerves and red blood cells healthy and helps make DNA.
  • Vitamin C (Ascorbic acid)Needed for collagen, wound healing and immunity, and boosts iron absorption.
  • Vitamin D (Calciferol)Helps you absorb calcium and keeps bones and muscles strong.
  • Vitamin E (Tocopherol)An antioxidant that protects your cells from damage.
  • Vitamin K (Phylloquinone)Needed for blood clotting and healthy bones.
  • CholineSupports the brain, liver and muscles.
  • LycopeneA red plant pigment and antioxidant, richest in cooked tomatoes.

Minerals

  • CalciumBuilds bones and teeth and helps muscles, nerves and the heart work.
  • PhosphorusBuilds bones and teeth and helps store and use energy.
  • MagnesiumInvolved in muscles, nerves, blood sugar, blood pressure and energy.
  • PotassiumBalances fluids and supports healthy blood pressure and heart rhythm.
  • ChlorideBalances fluids with sodium and potassium, and makes stomach acid.
  • IronCarries oxygen in your blood and muscles.
  • ZincSupports immunity, wound healing, growth, taste and smell.
  • CopperHelps make red blood cells and supports nerves, immunity and energy.
  • ManganeseHelps with bone formation, metabolism and antioxidant defenses.
  • SeleniumSupports antioxidant enzymes, the thyroid and immunity.
  • IodineNeeded to make thyroid hormones, which set how fast your body uses energy.
  • FluorideHardens tooth enamel and helps protect teeth from decay.
  • ChromiumHelps insulin move sugar from your blood into your cells.

Macronutrients

  • ProteinBuilds and repairs muscle, skin, enzymes and hormones.
  • CarbohydratesYour body’s main fuel, especially for the brain and hard exercise.
  • FatSupplies energy and essential fats, and helps you absorb vitamins A, D, E and K.

Carbohydrates and fiber

  • FiberFeeds your gut bacteria and supports digestion, fullness, cholesterol and blood sugar.
  • StarchThe main carbohydrate in grains, potatoes, beans and bananas, and a steady source of energy.
  • Soluble fiberFiber that dissolves into a gel, slowing digestion and lowering cholesterol.
  • Insoluble fiberFiber that doesn’t dissolve. It adds bulk and keeps things moving through your gut.

Fats

  • Monounsaturated fatA heart-friendly fat found in olive oil, avocados and most nuts.
  • Polyunsaturated fatIncludes the essential omega-3 and omega-6 fats. Found in seeds, nuts, fish and vegetable oils.
  • Omega-3 ALA (Alpha-linolenic acid)An essential fat from plants, used for energy and to make other omega-3s.
  • Omega-3 EPA + DHAHealthy fats for the heart, brain and eyes.
  • Omega-6 (Linoleic acid)An essential fat for healthy skin and cell membranes.
