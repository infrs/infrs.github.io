Foods high in insoluble fiber

Fiber that doesn’t dissolve. It adds bulk and keeps things moving through your gut. The richest common sources per serving are navy beans (cooked), guava and split peas (cooked).

What insoluble fiber does

Insoluble fiber passes through your gut mostly whole, holding water and adding bulk, so food moves along and you stay regular. It’s in wheat bran, whole grains, nuts, seeds and the skins of fruit and vegetables.

How much you need

No set amount: most fiber is this kind

Tips

  • Drink enough water as you eat more fiber, or it can make you constipated.

Good to know

  • Most foods have both kinds; about two thirds to three quarters of the fiber in a typical diet is insoluble.
