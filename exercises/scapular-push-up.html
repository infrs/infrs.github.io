Scapular Push-Up

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Beginner
Type
Strength
Movement
Isolation (one joint)
Force
Push

Especially targets

  • Middle pectoralis majorSternocostal head, the bulk of the chest
  • Serratus anteriorAlong the ribs, below the armpit

Main muscles

  • Chest

Also works

  • Shoulders

How to do it

  1. Start in a push-up position with straight arms.
  2. Without bending your elbows, let your chest sink so your shoulder blades squeeze together.
  3. Push the floor away so your shoulder blades spread apart and your upper back rounds slightly.
  4. Repeat slowly. Only your shoulder blades move.

Trains the serratus anterior, which keeps your shoulder blades healthy for pressing.
