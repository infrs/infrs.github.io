Body Atlas: training and nutrition

Tap a muscle, bone or organ on a 3D model of the human body, then see the exercises that train it and the foods rich in the nutrients it needs.

Free, with optional support

The app is free to use. You can watch an optional video ad to unlock a colour theme, or buy a supporter badge or leave a tip if you want to help the app along.

Not medical advice

Body Atlas gives general fitness and nutrition information. It is not medical advice and is no substitute for a doctor, dietitian or qualified trainer. Talk to a professional before changing your exercise or diet, especially if you have a health condition.
