TRX Dips

Equipment
Other
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Especially targets

  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)Outer back of the arm, the "horseshoe"

Main muscles

  • Triceps

Also works

  • Chest

How to do it

  1. Start with the hands on the suspension trainer and feet on the ground.
  2. Your hands should be under your shoulders with the feet slightly in front.
  3. Lower yourself by bringing the hips between the handles and flexing the elbows.
  4. Extend the elbows to come back to the starting position and repeat.
