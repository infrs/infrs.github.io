Tensor fasciae latae: what it does and how to train it

Front of the hip, at the side; helps lift the leg out. The best exercises for it include Thigh Abductor, Side-Lying Hip Abduction and Clamshell.

What it does

A small muscle at the front of the hip that pulls on the IT band, a long strip of tough tissue down the outer thigh. It helps lift the leg forward and out, and steadies the hip and knee.

Best exercises for the tensor fasciae latae

  1. Thigh AbductorMachine · beginner · isolation
  2. Side-Lying Hip AbductionNo equipment · beginner · isolation
  3. ClamshellNo equipment · beginner · isolation
  4. Monster WalkBands · beginner · compound
