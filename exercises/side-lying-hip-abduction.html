Main muscles

  • Outer hips

Also works

  • Glutes

How to do it

  1. Lie on your side with your legs straight and stacked, head resting on your arm.
  2. Turn the toes of your top leg slightly down and keep that leg in line with your body.
  3. Lift the top leg up to about 45°, leading with the heel.
  4. Lower slowly. Finish all reps, then switch sides.
