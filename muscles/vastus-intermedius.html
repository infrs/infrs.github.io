Vastus intermedius: what it does and how to train it

Under the rectus femoris, against the thigh bone. The best exercises for it include Barbell Squat, Front Squat (Clean Grip) and Bulgarian Split Squat.

What it does

Lies right against the thigh bone, under the rectus femoris and between the other two vasti. It can’t be seen or felt from outside, but it straightens the knee in every squat, lunge and leg extension.

Best exercises for the vastus intermedius

  1. Barbell SquatBarbell · beginner · compound
  2. Front Squat (Clean Grip)Barbell · intermediate · compound
  3. Bulgarian Split SquatNo equipment · intermediate · compound
  4. Leg PressMachine · beginner · compound
  5. Hack SquatMachine · beginner · compound
  6. Leg ExtensionsMachine · beginner · isolation
