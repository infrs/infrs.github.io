Banded Clamshell

Equipment
Bands
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength

Main muscles

  • Glutes
  • Abs

Also works

  • Quads

How to do it

  1. Position Side plank with the elbow and the forearm on the ground and the legs slightly bent.
  2. Stretch a doubled-up resistance band between your thighs, just above the knee.
  3. Execution Lift your upper knee while keeping your feet close together, like a “shell opening,” then slowly return to the starting position.
  4. Keep your core stable and your hips aligned.
