Lower rectus abdominis: what it does and how to train it

The six-pack below the navel. The best exercises for it include Hanging Leg Raise, Hanging Knee Raise and Flat Bench Lying Leg Raise.

What it does

The part of the six-pack below the navel. Moves that curl the pelvis up toward the ribs, like reverse crunches and hanging leg raises, tend to favor it.

Best exercises for the lower rectus abdominis

  1. Hanging Leg RaiseOther · expert · isolation
  2. Hanging Knee RaiseOther · beginner · isolation
  3. Flat Bench Lying Leg RaiseNo equipment · beginner · isolation
  4. Dead BugNo equipment · beginner · compound
  5. Bent-Knee Hip RaiseNo equipment · beginner · compound
  6. Bottoms UpNo equipment · beginner · compound
