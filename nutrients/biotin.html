Foods high in vitamin B7 (biotin)

Helps turn fat, carbs and protein into energy, and supports hair, skin and nails. The richest common sources per serving are chicken liver (cooked), beef liver (cooked) and soybeans (cooked). The Daily Value is 30 µg a day for adults.

What vitamin B7 (biotin) does

Biotin helps the enzymes that break down fat, carbohydrates and protein for energy, and the ones that build fatty acids. Your gut bacteria make some too, so a true shortage is rare.

How much you need

The Daily Value is 30 µg a day for adults.

Tips

  • Eggs, liver, salmon, seeds and nuts are good sources.
  • Raw egg white holds a protein (avidin) that locks biotin away; cooking the egg breaks it down.

Good to know

  • High-dose biotin supplements can throw off some blood tests, including thyroid and heart tests. Tell your doctor if you take them.
  • There’s no upper limit: extra biotin leaves in urine.
