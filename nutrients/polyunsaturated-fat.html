Foods high in polyunsaturated fat

Includes the essential omega-3 and omega-6 fats. Found in seeds, nuts, fish and vegetable oils. The richest common sources per serving are walnuts, hemp seeds and pine nuts.

What polyunsaturated fat does

Polyunsaturated fats include the omega-3 and omega-6 fats your body can’t make. Eating them instead of saturated fat lowers LDL cholesterol and is linked to fewer heart attacks.

Tips

  • Keep nut oils, nuts and seeds cool and dark: polyunsaturated fats go rancid faster than other fats.

Good to know

  • Walnuts are unusual among nuts: most of their fat is polyunsaturated.
