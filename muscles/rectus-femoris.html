Rectus femoris: what it does and how to train it

Middle of the front thigh; also lifts the leg. The best exercises for it include Leg Extensions, Reverse Nordic and Weighted Sissy Squat.

What it does

Runs straight down the middle of the front thigh and is the only quadriceps muscle that crosses the hip. So besides straightening the knee, it lifts the thigh, as in leg raises and kicks.

Best exercises for the rectus femoris

  1. Leg ExtensionsMachine · beginner · isolation
  2. Reverse NordicNo equipment · intermediate · isolation
  3. Weighted Sissy SquatBarbell · expert · compound
  4. Hip Flexion with BandBands · beginner · compound
  5. Single-Leg Leg ExtensionMachine · beginner · isolation
  6. Sissy SquatNo equipment · intermediate · isolation
