How to do it

  1. Jump or climb to the top of a straight-bar dip, arms straight above the bar.
  2. Lower slowly into the bottom of the dip, keeping your chest over the bar.
  3. Lean back and let your wrists roll over the bar as your chest drops under it, still controlling the descent.
  4. Lower through a pull-up to a full hang, taking 3–5 seconds in all.

Go slowest through the middle, where the dip turns into the pull-up: that’s the part a muscle-up needs most.
