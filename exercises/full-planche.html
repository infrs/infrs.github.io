How to do it

  1. From a straddle planche, bring your legs together behind you.
  2. Hold your whole body straight and level with the floor, held up only by your straight arms.
  3. Lean far enough that your legs balance your shoulders, which end up well in front of your hands.
  4. Hold, then lower your feet with control.

Most people take years of steady training to get here. Train the earlier steps two or three times a week, and give your wrists, elbows and shoulders time to adapt.
