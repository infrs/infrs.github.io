Reverse Nordic

Equipment
No equipment
Level
Intermediate
Type
Strength
Movement
Isolation (one joint)
Force
Push

Especially targets

  • Rectus femorisMiddle of the front thigh; also lifts the leg

Main muscles

  • Quads

Also works

  • Abs

How to do it

  1. Kneel on a soft surface with your knees hip width apart and your body upright.
  2. Keeping a straight line from your knees to your head, lean back slowly by letting your knees bend.
  3. Go only as far as you can control.
  4. Squeeze your thighs to pull yourself back upright.

Stretches and strengthens the front of the thigh, including the rectus femoris.
