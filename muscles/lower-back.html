Best lower back exercises

Barbell Deadlift, Hyperextensions (Back Extensions) and Good Morning are among the best exercises for the lower back. Below are the top picks with start and end pictures, and the muscles that make up the lower back.

What the lower back muscles do

The erector spinae are three columns of muscle along each side of the spine. They straighten the back, hold you upright and keep the spine stable when you bend, lift or carry.
