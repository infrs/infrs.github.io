How to do it

  1. Start in a push-up position, then walk your feet in and lift your hips so your body makes an upside-down V.
  2. Keep your legs as straight as you comfortably can and your head between your arms.
  3. Bend your elbows to lower the top of your head toward the floor in front of your hands.
  4. Push back up until your arms are straight.
